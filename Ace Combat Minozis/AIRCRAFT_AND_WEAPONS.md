# Aircraft and Weapons

The playable roster is limited to aircraft established in the project and present in Strangereal during this period. The roster is small by series standards because the campaign is built around specific pilots in specific aircraft.

Related: [Flight and Combat](FLIGHT_AND_COMBAT.md) · [Progression and Hangar](PROGRESSION_AND_HANGAR.md) · [MNZ-41](../WORLD_AND_LORE/TECHNOLOGY/MNZ-41_MINOZIS.md)

| **F-35C Lightning II** | Thorne | Multirole stealth; sensor fusion | Wins before the merge; the most information, the least forgiveness |

## 3. Weapons

### Standard

| Weapon | Notes |
|---|---|
| Gun | All aircraft. The F-35A's GAU-22/A is the weapon of the prologue and the finale |
| Standard missile (MSL) | All aircraft; locked out near civilians |

### Special weapons

Each aircraft carries one of three special weapons, chosen in the hangar.

| Aircraft | Options |
|---|---|
| F-35C | Long-range AAM · Multi-target AAM · Guided bombs |
| F/A-18E | Multi-target AAM · Short-range high-maneuver AAM · Guided air-to-surface missiles |
| Rafale M | Long-range AAM · Multi-target AAM · Guided air-to-surface missiles |
| F-35A | Long-range AAM · Short-range high-maneuver AAM · Guided bombs |
| EF-2000B | Meteor-class BVRAAM · IRIS-T-class short-range AAM · Taurus-class cruise missile (Free Mission only) |

Special weapons follow series conventions. No special weapon damages the MNZ-41 outside a window beyond chip damage.
