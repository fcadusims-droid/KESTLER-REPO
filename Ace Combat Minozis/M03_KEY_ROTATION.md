## 1. Narrative Purpose

The squadron fights the enemy's puppets without ever seeing the enemy. Asano's perception is the only thing that notices a fifth party in the swarm. The mission plants dramatic irony: the player will learn in M04 what took the drones.

## 2. Briefing

> The Erusean military is conducting a counter-proliferation drill with a 162-drone swarm and has invited ISAF Revival observers as a confidence-building measure. Six-AME will fly observation and act as a safety escort for crewed aircraft in the drill area. Weapons are tight. This is a diplomatic sortie.

## 3. Objectives

| Type | Objective |
|---|---|
| Primary (initial) | Observe the drill; maintain separation from the swarm |
| Primary (updated) | Defend the launch base until the swarm's fuel runs out |
| Secondary | Protect the base's crewed aircraft on the apron |
| Hidden | Identify the fifth handshake before the swarm turns |

## 4. Flow

**Phase 1: Observation.** Slow, formal flight. Asano's Read display shows the swarm's handshake traffic as four distinct key-holder signatures cycling during a scheduled key rotation. Thirty seconds into the rotation a fifth signature appears. Only the Read display shows it.

If the player marks it (hidden objective), Asano reports it:

> **NINE:** "Over, I've got five voices in a four-voice choir."
> **OVER:** "Erusean control reports nominal."
> **NINE:** "Then Erusean control is the fourth voice and somebody else is singing louder."

Nobody acts on it either way.

**Phase 2: The turn.** 158 drones break formation and dive on their own launch base. Four drones on a different key go inert. Weapons are released.

**Phase 3: Defense.** Swarm defense at close range over a base under attack. The drones attack in waves timed to the base's own defensive fire patterns. Asano's Read display predicts each wave from handshake timing; the player can relay predictions to the flight with the Cover order.

**Phase 4: Exhaustion.** The swarm runs out of fuel. Survivors crash into the sea or the perimeter.

## 5. Key Radio

> **SIX:** "Somebody's running these like they read our playbook."
> **AME:** "Or like they read theirs."

## 7. Failure Conditions

- Base command center destroyed.
- More than half the apron aircraft destroyed.
- Asano shot down.

## 8. Debrief and Circle

An Erusean liaison officer thanks the squadron and files the event as a training accident. Asano says in the circle that she does not believe it; for once nobody thinks she is joking.
