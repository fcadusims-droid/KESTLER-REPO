# Canon Foundation

This document states which parts of the setting come from Ace Combat canon and how the project builds on them. Sources are listed in [Canon Sources](CANON_SOURCES.md). Fixed facts are restated in the [Continuity Bible](CONTINUITY_BIBLE.md).

---

## 1. A Spin-off Between the Numbered Games

*Ace Combat MINOZIS* is a spin-off of the Ace Combat franchise. It occupies the decade between *Ace Combat 7: Skies Unknown* (the Lighthouse War, 2019) and *Ace Combat 8: Wings of Theve* (the Sotoa–Usea War, from 16 July 2029), and its consequences extend into the 2030s that lead to *Ace Combat 3: Electrosphere* (2040). The campaign ends on 18 June 2029, twenty-eight days before Sotoa's opening strike on Expo City.

| Years | Canon event | Game | Relation to *MINOZIS* |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1995 | Belkan War | *Ace Combat Zero* | Origin of Gründer Industries' Z.O.E. program |
| 1997–1998 | Usean Coup | *Ace Combat 2*, *Assault Horizon Legacy* | Z.O.E. defeated by a human pilot; the precedent the Directorate ignores |
| 2010 | Circum-Pacific War | *Ace Combat 5* | Gorev's wound; the Grey Men; Asano's flight from Dresdene |
| 2015–2016 | Emmeria–Estovakia War | *Ace Combat 6* | Thorne's first war |
| 2019 | Lighthouse War | *Ace Combat 7* | The satellite blackout, the drone lineage and the reconstruction debt that shape the setting |
| 2020 | Aurelian War | *Ace Combat X* | Concurrent canon conflict elsewhere; not part of this story |
| **2019–2029** | **The hidden decade** | ***Ace Combat MINOZIS*** | **MINOZIS built (2025), reactivated (2028), forced down (June 2029)** |
| 2029–2030 | Sotoa–Usea War | *Ace Combat 8* | Begins 28 days after the finale; ends Enso Order and the Task Force's surveillance posture |

| 2040 | Intercorporate War | *Ace Combat 3* | The corporate world the decade leads to |

### What a spin-off may and may not do

| May | May not |
|---|---|
| Use its own cast, units and operations | Change the outcome of any numbered game |
| Show canon events from a different angle (the Lighthouse War's blackout, Sotoa's build-up, the Rocky Island "accident") | Put its characters into scenes or missions from the numbered games |
| Explain things canon leaves open, where no canon fact contradicts it | Name or involve canon protagonists (Trigger, Rex, Joker Flight) |
| Point forward to canon outcomes in epilogue text | Show events after June 2029 except as canon-horizon text |

### Why this gap

Canon shows the period from 2019 to 2040 as the slow transfer of power from states to corporations, ending in the corporate world of *Ace Combat 3: Electrosphere*. The project tells one hidden piece of that transfer: how a state's secret weapon became a datum in a corporation's feasibility study for wiring a human into a machine. In canon, that study is General Resource's Darkness of Enigma, and it ends with a child prodigy undergoing artificial nerve surgery. The project never shows that outcome. It shows the four years that made it possible.

## 3. Canon Constraints and How the Project Honors Them

**No war between 2019 and July 2029.** Canon has no declared war in this window. The project's 2028 events form a *crisis*: assassination attempts, cyber attacks, deniable engagements and one orbital clash, all absorbed by cover stories. MINOZIS reactivates because its activation condition reads crisis indicators as war. It cannot tell the difference, and that failure is the point.

**Osea and Yuktobania are at peace in 2029.** Canon confirms both are superpowers whose intervention in Usea was still only a possibility in 2030. The project keeps them at peace: their Arctic dispute is a standoff of overlapping patrol zones, and the Counter-MINOZIS Task Force is the joint instrument of two governments that agreed to lie together.

**The orbital layer works again by 2029.** The 2019 war destroyed both superpowers' satellite networks and blacked out the world's information networks, but canon shows the International Space Elevator's solar-power satellites, Enso's Comona launches and the Tonitrus Satellite in operation by 2029. The project treats polar navigation as the slowest service to recover, which is why the Arctic boundaries stayed ambiguous through 2025.

**Neucom is never named.** Canon says the privatized EASA's joint venture with an airline became Neucom but gives no year. The campaign refers only to the privatized EASA.

**No COFFIN combat is known before 2030.** Canon records the first *known* combat flight of a COFFIN-type aircraft on 24 March 2030, near Cima Island. MINOZIS fits only because none of its engagements ever became known: every one is absorbed by [the Agreed Fiction](THE_AGREED_FICTION.md), and the finale's wreck lies under the ice.

**Yuktobania and Osea are on ceremonial terms.** In June 2020 Yuktobania attended the Belkan War's 25th-anniversary ceremony at Osea's invitation. The Arctic rivalry of the project grows inside that cordial public relationship.

**General Resource is ambitious, not dominant.** Canon places GR's rise to global prominence after it inherits Enso's assets in 2030. Before that it is already a private security power in Usea: in 2024 it bought out In-sa-net Telegraph and Telephone Communication, and in 2026 its Defense Force destroyed a terrorist organization at the request of Usean governments that had lost much of their own power. It is not yet the hegemon it becomes.

**Enso's guilt stays hidden.** Enso is a decentralized autonomous organization whose nodes can operate without knowing each other's work. Its Planning Division 303's role in prolonging the war is exposed only in 2030. Characters in 2029 see Enso as a lender, a launch provider and Morgenstern's corporate owner.

## 5. Canon Horizon

The campaign ends before the Sotoa–Usea War. The following canon events happen afterward and frame the ending:

- Recovery operations over the MINOZIS wreck stop when the war begins on 16 July 2029.
- On 24 March 2030 the first *known* COFFIN combat flight takes place near Cima Island.
- In April 2030 Enso's Operation Sweep strikes its own facilities, including Okchabursk, to destroy evidence.
- On 31 August 2030 Enso Order dissolves and General Resource inherits its Usean assets, DOE included. Morgenstern's data and any surviving nodes pass to GR.
- DOE reaches its living subject in the early 2030s; the X-49 first flies on 21 July 2032; GR ends DOE in 2033 and its staff defect to Neucom.

The dossier's warning that "a corporation that cannot obtain a dead pilot's neural interface data will build its own experiment" is therefore fulfilled in canon. The campaign stops MINOZIS. It does not stop the feasibility study, and the ending acknowledges this.
