## 1. Background

Born in the Dresdene region of southeastern Yuktobania, mountains and villages known mainly for an engineering college. On 2 November 2010 aircraft in Osean markings struck that college. Two days later Yuktobanian terrorists released gas in Bana City, an Osean college town south of Oured. Her family had signed a district petition for a negotiated settlement. Within the month they fled to Osea, arriving as refugees from the country that had just gassed Osean civilians, into the country still counting them.

The Dresdene strike was later revealed to be a Grey Men false flag. By then it did not matter to the children on her street, for whom *Bana* was a word that could be said to a ten-year-old to make her stop talking.

Her parents found work in an electronics-component factory on the coast. She entered the ISAF Aviation Academy at North Point in 2022 on an academic-merit scholarship, the first in her family to complete higher education.

## 2. Psychology

**Dominant trait:** performative optimism, charisma maintained as armor. She reads emotional states acutely and hides it behind a deliberately shallow presentation, because a history of exclusion taught her that visible seriousness invites rejection.

**Formative event.** In 2014, four years after arriving, she won a school essay contest on hope during reconstruction. At the public ceremony she overheard a parent say, loud enough to carry, that the prize was post-war propaganda handed to a refugee for appearances. She has never since accepted recognition publicly without deflecting it with a joke.

**Resulting bias:** demonstrated intelligence or visible investment provokes resentment; the beloved clown survives where the competent outsider is resented. It produces validational dependency: she reads every expression for rejection, which makes her exceptional at reading people and leaves her chronically drained.

**Operational prognosis.** Tactically reliable; the concern is attrition, not failure. She carries the squadron's morale alone and it is never replenished. The likely break is a situation her humor cannot defuse, followed by someone she trusts telling her plainly that she does not have to perform to be valued. Against MINOZIS her perceptiveness is an asset; a system modeling operators would read her performance as a thin layer over a deep wound and press on it.

## 3. Voice

Bright, fast, teasing; a private vocabulary with Marsh that no one else fully understands. She sings melancholy Yuktobanian songs only when she believes nobody is listening.

## 4. Anchor

The callsign Nine, a Yuktobanian lucky number and the only public thread to her origin, which also implies completeness, pointedly ironic for someone who has felt incomplete since the crossing. The pink trim on her Super Hornet and the streaks in her hair: pink is the one color absent from the gray and olive of every war she has lived through. A flight-jacket patch with real rhinestones on the pink flame, which Thorne permits without acknowledging.

## 5. Aircraft and Playstyle

She flies the same F/A-18E as Marsh so that the two operate as one distributed instrument. Her airframe is the only visibly non-regulation aircraft on the deck, and her squadron leader has quietly ruled it within tolerance. It is the most public statement she allows herself: the refugee they tried to make invisible is flying a war machine they cannot fail to see.
