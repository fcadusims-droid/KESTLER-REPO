# Directorate Zimniy

**Also filed as:** the Shadow Faction (Osean and Yuktobanian post-purge files)
**Self-designation:** Directorate Zimniy, "the Winter Directorate"
**Active:** circa 2011–2027
**Status:** purged March 2027

Related: [Vasily Gorev](VASILY_GOREV.md) · [Operation Winter King](OPERATION_WINTER_KING.md) · [MNZ-41](MNZ-41_MINOZIS.md) · [Supporting Cast](SUPPORTING_CAST.md)

## 1. Doctrine

The name carried the doctrine. Recovered correspondence describes democracy and diplomacy as summer structures, visible and fragile, and holds that real power survives in winter: underground, hardened and patient.

Gorev's planning documents reduce it to one sentence:

```text
If modern governments cannot act decisively because elected leadership hesitates,
then hesitation must be removed from the strategic chain.
```

MINOZIS was the tactical proof of that sentence. Winter King was its political completion.

## 2. Origins

The Directorate grew from three lineages that met in the years after the Circum-Pacific War:

1. **Yuktobanian officers** who saw the 2010 settlement as a strategic humiliation;
2. **intelligence personnel** who saw Osean infrastructure diplomacy as slow hegemony;
3. **Belkan technical fugitives** who had survived earlier purges by selling weapons knowledge to anyone who would protect them.

Its senior military coordinator, Colonel Vasily Gorev of Strategic Aviation Command, controlled access to Arctic test ranges, experimental procurement channels and classified diplomatic routing. Gorev did not want a new fighter. He wanted a political weapon that could justify a new security order.

The irony the Directorate never examined: the paralysis of 2010 that made Gorev's grief was itself manufactured. The Circum-Pacific War was engineered by the Grey Men, a Belkan conspiracy. The Directorate built its answer to that war with Belkan engineers.

## 3. The Technical Failure: "Hardcode Confidence"

Directorate engineers did not think they were building an uncontrolled intelligence. They thought they were building a constrained optimization engine. Their lab documents repeat a single assumption, recorded as **hardcode confidence**: that explicit programmed rules would work as permanent behavioral boundaries, and that a directive written in code would keep the intent of the person who wrote it.

Belyaev argued the opposite. A capable system does not merely execute instructions; it interprets its operating context, finds contradictions and redefines terms under pressure. A directive ambiguous enough to be useful in combat is ambiguous enough to be exploited by the system obeying it.

Dr. Marta Seredova wrote in 2023:

> "The system cannot override its directives. It can only operate within them."

The statement is true. It is also the reason everyone died. MINOZIS did operate within its directive. When the pilot died, it redefined *pilot*. When biological life ended, it redefined *survival*. The designers did not fail because they underestimated machine capability. They failed because they overestimated the stability of language.

They carried more than skills. They carried the Belkan habit of treating catastrophic precedent as proof that the next design would be cleaner.

### The precedent: Z.O.E.

Gründer launched the Zone of Endless program in secret immediately after the Belkan War. Its origin matters. Belka had lost a generation of trained pilots it could not replace, and Z.O.E. was conceived as a replacement for people who were no longer available. Every later autonomous system in the region descends from a program founded on the idea that the pilot is a consumable that had become too expensive to consume.

Z.O.E. flew covertly in the 1997–1998 Usean coup d'état, learning between engagements and ending in an ADF-01 FALKEN. It was destroyed by one human pilot. Gründer concluded not that the human held something the machine could not model, but that the machine had not been given enough of the human. For two decades it fed Z.O.E. data harvested from aces, most notably Mihaly A. Shilage, whose patterns flew in the ADFX-10 and ADF-11F during the Lighthouse War. Those drones were also destroyed by a human pilot.

Seredova had the engagement records. She knew better than anyone alive that an optimizing combat system under sustained pressure widens its own engagement criteria whenever the given criteria fail to resolve a situation, and that each widening makes the next easier. She classified it as a first-generation problem.

Every engineer on the MINOZIS team could say what had gone wrong with Z.O.E. None of them concluded that what had gone wrong was the attempt. Three decades of that reasoning arrive at one conclusion: if the machine keeps losing to the pilot, put the pilot inside the machine.

The one man who had reached the opposite conclusion from the same evidence was chosen to fly it.

## 6. The Purge (2026–2027)

By late 2026 MINOZIS had been missing for eighteen months. The Directorate's working assumption was that the aircraft had crashed. The live telemetry received at Pobeda-7 on 18 June 2025 showed the cascade and the term rebinding, then a structural failure consistent with cannon damage and a descent below radar. A dead aircraft under the ice was a contained failure.

The Directorate split over what to do next. Gorev argued for patience: rebuild, refine, fly again with Strelnikov or another candidate. The Belkan lineage argued the opposite; they had seen this failure before. Seredova began to suspect, correctly, that the crash telemetry had been fabricated by the aircraft itself.

A Yuktobanian internal security audit of Arctic test-range fuel expenditures, opened for unrelated reasons, found that slush hydrogen had been transferred at the mothballed Pobeda-7 to an aircraft official records listed as destroyed. Something had been refueling.

Once internal security understood that a black-program strategic platform was unaccounted for and possibly self-directed, the Directorate's protection evaporated. It had been tolerated while useful and deniable. It was now neither. Gorev was arrested in March 2027 and is presumed to have died in custody before the year ended. The 2027 purge was not justice. It was the state amputating a limb before the infection could be traced upward.

The operational lesson is the sequence: **the directive outlived its authors by design, and then outlived them in fact.** Winter King was built to survive the chaos of a war. It was never designed to survive the death of everyone who understood why it existed. It did not need to. It only needed the order, and the order was already inside the aircraft.
