## 1. Foundational Injury

Her mother died in childbirth. Her father, Dr. Hans Lichtträger, raised her in isolation at a mountain research facility. In 2010, when she was five, Hans was arrested in the post-SOLG purges. She spent thirty-three months in state care, where caregivers recorded her spending hours at windows watching sunlight and starlight. She told one of them, "Shadows lie. Light doesn't."

When Hans returned in 2013 she held onto his coat through the entire release process. He became the only person she trusted. Then he taught her that needing people was weakness.

## 2. Education as Conditioning

Hans did not abuse her in crude terms. He trained her into isolation with precision: orbital mechanics, optics, solar radiation pressure, command architecture, neural interface theory, directed energy, military ethics filtered through utilitarian calculus, political game theory.

At eleven, after she solved an orbital problem, he told her: "You understood in weeks what took me years." She asked: "Does that mean you won't leave again?" He answered: "You'll never need anyone to stay. You'll be too important to abandon."

Competence became protection against abandonment. Control became love without risk.

## 3. The Illumination Incident (1 December 2024)

During a 24-node prototype test, the thermal model failed to account for subsurface conduction. Twelve technicians died in an overheated underground observation bunker. Althéa secured the telemetry servers during the evacuation and was burned doing it.

> "I saved the data servers. All telemetry intact."

Hans told her twelve people were dead.

> "Yes. I know. The system worked. The thermal model needs refinement."

Mandatory psychiatric treatment was recommended. Hans overrode it. She was decorated for composure.

## 4. Doctrine

She is not insane in any operational sense. She is coherent inside a closed moral system.

| Tenet | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Light as Truth | Visibility eliminates deception; stealth is moral cowardice |
| Fire as Judgment | Orbital fire is impartial because it falls from above politics |
| Awe as Peace | A world too afraid to fight is a world pacified |

She does not see that the judgment is still hers.

## 5. Where the Profile Fails

Almost everything known about her childhood was recorded by her father, who has spent twenty years building an account in which his daughter is the coherent product of a rigorous education. A profile built from a manipulator's records describes a person who makes sense, because making sense of her was the manipulator's project. Three facts do not fit.

**She lies about visibility.** Morgenstern spent years behind a cover story describing a passive mirror constellation, and she maintained that cover in briefings and in writing under her own name. When a Belkan liaison officer raised the contradiction in early 2028, she did not defend it. She declined to engage and changed the subject to orbital mechanics with a fluency he described as "practiced, the way a person is practiced at a door they do not open."

**She refused a valid target.** In November 2028 the constellation converged over a legitimate military target sold by an Enso planning node, under conditions her doctrine calls ideal: clear sky, daylight, adequate warning, a destruction visible for a hundred kilometers. She held the dwell and did not fire. No explanation exists anywhere. The target was struck by conventional means eleven days later.

**She keeps something with no doctrinal justification.** Maintenance imagery of her cockpit shows a child's mitten, red wool, in a side pocket where personal effects are not permitted. It is not her father's. It appears in no account of her childhood. One hypothesis links it to one of the twelve technicians, several of whom had children; another says she acquired it later and built its meaning afterward. Neither has evidence. She has never mentioned it. Ground crews have learned not to.

She is not the closed system her doctrine describes. Something in her still refuses, and something still keeps. That is why she is more dangerous than a true believer: a closed system is predictable, and a person defending a closed system against her own contradictions is not.

## 6. The Engagement and After

On 17 August 2028 MINOZIS destroyed between 22 and 28 of her satellites. She named each one and marked it with a black X. She felt them as phantom limbs: "He took my light. The ghost in the machine took pieces of me." She did not fly for six weeks. When she came back, she spoke to the satellites instead of the ground crew.

## 8. Writing Althéa

- Formal, exact, often impersonal; uses technical vocabulary as distance.
- Refers to satellites by name; to people by function.
- When cornered emotionally, she changes the subject to physics.
- Her rare first-person admissions are short and immediately retracted.
