# Canon Sources

All canon facts in this project are verified against a single source: the **Ace Combat Wiki** at [acecombat.wiki.gg](https://acecombat.wiki.gg/). No other source is used for lore research. The wiki's pages cite the games, official guidebooks and Bandai Namco material; those citations are recorded below as the wiki gives them.

Related: [Canon Foundation](CANON_FOUNDATION.md) · [Continuity Bible](CONTINUITY_BIBLE.md)

| Point | What the wiki records | Project choice |
|---|---|---|
| Who runs Darkness of Enigma in 2028–2029 | GR's program (DOE and GR pages); started by Enso with GR and former Gründer engineers (COFFIN page); an Enso asset inherited by GR (Enso page) | Run by GR with Enso participation; passes wholly to GR in 2030 |
| Rena Hirose's joining year | 2030 (GR page; consistent with joining at nine, born 2021) or 2031 (DOE page) | "Early 2030s" |
| Start of the Sotoa–Yuktobanian War | "Early 21st century"; "around the 2010s"; *Aces At War* says "the early 2000s" | "An earlier war with Yuktobania" |
| Neucom's incorporation year | Not given | Neucom is never named during the campaign |
| Space debris after the 2019 ASAT strikes | Not mentioned; the wiki records the destruction of the satellite networks and a worldwide blackout | "The 2019 blackout"; slow polar-navigation recovery is a project choice |
