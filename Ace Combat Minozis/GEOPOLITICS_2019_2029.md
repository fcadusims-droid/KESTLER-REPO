# Geopolitics, 2019–2029

The world that built MINOZIS was not at war. It was in debt, under-observed and full of weapons nobody had finished burying. This document describes that world from the end of the Lighthouse War to the eve of the Sotoa–Usea War.

## 1. The Restored Appearance of Diplomacy

The ceasefire of December 2019 restored the vocabulary of peace without its machinery. The post-Circum-Pacific framework between Osea and Yuktobania (the Harling–Nikanor declaration at Bright Hill in December 2010 and the arms reduction treaty that followed) remained in force on paper. Both governments reaffirmed restraint, mutual security and reconciliation in every public forum.

Operationally, three forces were pulling that framework apart.

## 2. The Blackout and the Ambiguous North

During the Lighthouse War, in September 2019, Osea and Erusea destroyed each other's satellite networks. The result was a worldwide blackout of satellite and information networks and months of chaos across Usea.

Most services were rebuilt within a few years. Polar navigation was rebuilt last, because it served the fewest paying customers. Through 2025 the Arctic still had coverage gaps in which positions could not be independently verified, and boundaries that had once been settled by satellite became matters of assertion.

At the same time, accelerated ice melt exposed rare-earth deposits, hydrocarbons, deep-water transit corridors and forward basing sites. Osea and Yuktobania each declared overlapping security zones around their northern installations. Each called the other's patrols provocations and its own patrols defensive. Each prepared the legal argument it would need if a crisis became useful.

The grid sector near **73.2°N, 142.8°E** became the most dangerous of these spaces: officially international, functionally contested, and administratively ideal for a test nobody would admit to. The Directorate Zimniy chose it for that reason.

## 3. Reconstruction Debt

Rebuilding after 2019 was not optional. Navigation, weather and communications capacity that every economy had assumed would always exist had to be replaced, and Usea's devastated cities had to be rebuilt. Sovereign credit was exhausted by the war, so the capital came from private lenders and from states acting through them.

Two patterns dominated.

**The eastern Usean debt trap.** From March 2020, Sotoa offered the nations of eastern Usea large loans and infrastructure projects through the conglomerate Enso Order. These nations had fallen behind the west, which drew energy from the International Space Elevator. The offers were accepted. Over the decade, eastern Usean communications, energy, transport and commerce became dependent on Sotoan capital. In November 2024 a partnership between a Sotoan company and Enso won the upgrade contract for the Comona Islands space center, giving Enso exclusive control of space development in eastern Usea.

**Corporate collateral elsewhere.** Beyond eastern Usea, reconstruction was financed by industrial and aerospace consortia that had spent the previous decade buying bankrupt manufacturers from every former combatant. General Resource was the largest. The privatized EASA was a smaller creditor. Their terms were commercially ordinary and strategically extraordinary: collateral in orbital slots, launch access, mineral concessions and, in several documented cases, long-term operating leases on infrastructure that had previously been military.

Creditors that hold sovereign collateral acquire an interest in whether the sovereign performs. By 2026 this had become visible to anyone who looked. Servicing the debt required export earnings. Export earnings required access to the deposits that polar melt had exposed and that collapsed satellite boundaries had made legally ambiguous. The Arctic dispute was therefore not really about territory. It was about solvency, conducted by governments that had already pledged the collateral they would need if they lost.

## 4. The Weapons Nobody Buried

Thirty years of conflict had scattered military autonomy across the world: Z.O.E.-derived control software, ADF-11F Ravens, MQ-101 drones, drone-control modules and the engineers who understood them. Former Gründer personnel sold their knowledge to whoever would protect them. Neo-Belkan cells pursued the old borderless vision with stolen hardware. Corporate proxies moved controlled technology through neutral corridors under refugee cover.

This is the threat environment the ISAF Revival Initiative was chartered to contain. See [ISAF Revival Initiative](FACTIONS/ISAF_REVIVAL_INITIATIVE.md).

## 5. The 2028 Crisis

No war was declared in 2028. What happened instead was a year of incidents that no government could explain without explaining too much.

## 6. The Sotoan Variable

Sotoa spent the post-Lighthouse decade doing what a creditor with an emergency does. Its homeland was drying out: rapid industrialization, accelerated by an earlier war with Yuktobania, had turned grassland to desert and made fresh water scarce. Its military leadership had committed to a long-term plan to settle in Usea. It held collateral over eastern Usea rather than owing it.

By March 2029 the indicators were visible, though their meaning was not:

- the Podarge heavy seaplane, which could cross from Sotoa to eastern Usea in two and a half hours;
- the Rocky Island port and shipyard, completed in October 2027 and sealed after a reported reactor accident in February 2028;
- forward fuel contracting on the Usean littoral and a mobilization tempo inconsistent with Sotoa's published exercise cycle;
- a "large space telescope" launched from Comona on 22 January 2029.

The March 2029 dossier assesses at Medium Confidence that Sotoa intends offensive action against the FCU within twelve months. It is correct. On 16 July 2029 the Tonitrus Spear struck Expo City.

Three consequences of that assessment shape the campaign:

1. **The theater will move.** A major Usean war would strip the persistent surveillance assets the Task Force depends on within weeks.
2. **The scoring function will find new targets.** A new war creates new commanders and new institutional weak points faster than any protection effort can cover them.
3. **The window is short.** The Task Force must end the aircraft before the theater changes. That conclusion drives the campaign toward [Operation ANCHOR](../MISSIONS/M09_ANCHOR_IN_THE_STORM.md) in June 2029.
