# Need for Speed Beyond

> **Unofficial fan concept.** *Need for Speed: Beyond* is a fan-made game concept written by **Johnny Kestler**. It is not a real product, not in development, and not affiliated with, authorised by, or endorsed by Electronic Arts Inc. or any of its studios. *Need for Speed* and all related titles referenced here — *Underground*, *Underground 2*, *Most Wanted*, *Carbon*, *ProStreet*, *Shift* — are trademarks of Electronic Arts Inc. All vehicle manufacturers, series and championships named here are the property of their respective owners and are referenced for illustrative purposes only. The author has no connection to Electronic Arts. This concept is written for its own sake, as a design exercise, and is distributed non-commercially.

*Need for Speed: Beyond* is a single-player racing game concept built on two halves of one car culture: the illegal street scene of **Nitroit**, an open-world American industrial city, and the **Revada Festival**, a months-long sanctioned racing festival a day's drive west. Its protagonist, Jake Spaid, is a trained junior driver who quit racing after his father died leading a professional championship. He conquers the streets alone, then has to assemble a team out of the people he met there to enter the one place he has been avoiding.
