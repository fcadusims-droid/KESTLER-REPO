# Glossary

One-line definitions. Each links to the file that owns the term.

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Act / Epilogue** | The campaign's six parts: Acts 1–5 in Nitroit, then the Epilogue, "Beyond", in which the festival opens. [Campaign](story/campaign.md) |
| **Blueprint** | A complete saved specification of a car; switching between Blueprints costs days and labour; the unit of the online layer. [Cars and building](systems/cars-and-building.md#blueprints) |
| **Blueprint Network** | The asynchronous online layer through which players share Blueprints. [Blueprint Network](systems/blueprint-network.md) |
| **Bounty** | The police value that orders the List; it only ever rises. [Police](systems/police.md#the-list) |
| **Builder Rank** | An online-only reputation earned from endorsements; no effect on the single-player game. [Blueprint Network](systems/blueprint-network.md#endorsements) |
| **Circuit Final** | The race at Sandreach, for team leaders only, that decides the championship outright. [Rank and championship](festival/rank-and-championship.md#the-circuit-final) |
| **Clock** | The in-game clock: one day every 24 real minutes, always running, jumping only in the travel and next-edition cutscenes. [Time](systems/time.md#the-clock) |
| **Commission** | The 10% of every buy-in in a district paid weekly to its leader. [Street scene](world/street-scene.md#leading-a-district) |
| **Class** | A car's performance bracket, D to X, computed from a single performance index. [Cars and building](systems/cars-and-building.md#vehicle-classes) |
| **Conduct multiplier** | The festival's scoring multiplier for clean or dirty driving. [Rank and championship](festival/rank-and-championship.md#scoring) |
| **Crew storyline** | Each crew leader's and unaffiliated racer's three-step personal story; one source of the final point to Loyal. [Crew storylines](story/crew-storylines.md) |
| **Crew** | The group that controls a Nitroit district and sets its rules. [Street scene](world/street-scene.md#districts-and-crews) |
| **District challenge** | A ranked racer's one-race challenge to the district's leader for control of it. [Street scene](world/street-scene.md#defending-a-district) |
| **Discipline** | One of the festival's five competitions: Grip, Drag, Drift, Speed, Sprint. [Disciplines](festival/disciplines.md) |
| **Driver Rank** | A driver's standing in one discipline's ladder; five separate tables, no overall in the regular season. [Rank and championship](festival/rank-and-championship.md#two-classifications) |
| **Expertise** | A mechanic's 1–5 rating, setting baseline setup, scrutineering, repair and tyre work. [Mechanics](festival/mechanics.md#expertise) |
| **Family ties** | The rule linking a crew leader's Standing to their family's mechanic. [Relationships](systems/relationships.md#family-ties) |
| **Grade** | The festival's ranking of a driver by his lowest-placed discipline, from Applicant to Festival Champion. [Rank and championship](festival/rank-and-championship.md#competition-grade) |
| **Heat** | Five-level police pursuit intensity. [Police](systems/police.md#heat) |
| **Insight** | A mechanic's legal, non-obvious optimisation, shared as Standing with him rises. [Mechanics](festival/mechanics.md#insights) |
| **Invitational** | An unsolicited, both-cars-staked endurance challenge from one of the city's nine best drivers. [Street events](systems/street-events.md#invitationals) |
| **King titles** | The five festival discipline titles (Grip King and so on). [Rank and championship](festival/rank-and-championship.md#discipline-titles) |
| **King of the Streets** | The highest REP title, reached at the end of Act 3. [Reputation](systems/reputation.md#titles) |
| **The List** | The police's ranking of the twenty most-wanted street racers, which decides what fleet they send. [Police](systems/police.md#the-list) |
| **Personality** | A racer's fixed temperament, one of six, deciding how they drive. [Relationships](systems/relationships.md#personality) |
| **Presentation** | A 1–6 rating of a build's visual and interior work; used only in the city. [Cars and building](systems/cars-and-building.md#presentation) |
| **REP** | The protagonist's reputation in the street scene as a whole. [Reputation](systems/reputation.md) |
| **Riverside check** | Riverside's published build-coherence list for entry. [Street scene](world/street-scene.md#riverside--riverside) |
| **Roaming racer** | A crew member driving freely around the city who can be challenged to an impromptu race. [Street events](systems/street-events.md#roaming-racers) |
| **Simulation layer** | What the game tracks about the car's condition while it is driven; arcade in the city, more simulated in the festival. [Driving and damage](systems/driving-and-damage.md#the-simulation-layer) |
| **Specialist discipline** | The one discipline in which a mechanic's Expertise counts one level higher and their Insights apply. [Mechanics](festival/mechanics.md#specialisation) |
| **Standing** | One NPC's relationship with the protagonist, from Hostile (−3) to Loyal (+3). Not to be confused with Team Standings. [Relationships](systems/relationships.md#standing) |
| **Team leader** | The one driver per all-discipline team who races everything and alone can start the Circuit Final; Jake leads his team. [Rank and championship](festival/rank-and-championship.md#team-leaders) |
| **Team Standings** | The festival's combined team table across all five disciplines. [Rank and championship](festival/rank-and-championship.md#two-classifications) |
| **Window** | The six to eight in-game days between two festival race dates, two of them travel. [Time](systems/time.md#during-a-season) |
