# Production Scope

The scope the design commits to. Platform targets, budget and market positioning are separate decisions and are not set here.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Structure** | Single-player campaign: five acts plus an open-ended Epilogue. See [Campaign](campaign.md). |
| **Campaign length** | 30 to 40 hours to the end of Act 5; 60 to 80 hours to the championship. |
| **Post-championship** | Uncapped. City and festival both remain fully open. See [Ending](ending.md#after-the-championship). |
| **Cars** | 110 to 140, across classes D to X, all road cars. Three starter options at the opening lot; none is ever awarded free. See [Cars and building](cars-and-building.md). |
| **Property** | Rentable and purchasable across all seven districts, garage capacity 0 to 12. See [Property](property.md). |
| **Licensing** | Assumes licensed real manufacturers. The class system, the district entry rules and Meridian's S/X restriction all depend on a real hypercar roster; fictional substitutes would weaken all three. |
| **Named NPCs** | Roughly 40: the named [cast](cast.md), plus crew members, magazine editors and festival rivals. |
| **Locations** | 7 districts in Nitroit; 10 festival venues across Revada. |
| **Festival layouts** | 18 to 24, drawn from the 10 Revada venues. |
| **Camera and control** | Entirely in-car. No on-foot movement at any point; every location is driven into. Jake appears outside the car only as a fixed pose in photography ([Magazines and Photo Mode](magazines-and-photo-mode.md)) and in cutscenes. |
| **Narrative delivery** | In-world and diegetic: garage dialogue, radio during events, conversation at night meets and in the paddock, magazines and television in the world. |
| **Cutscenes** | The only time the player is out of control of the car. Four story cutscenes — the opening sequence, the end of Act 5, the final sequence, and the post-credits scene — plus the travel sequence, which plays every time Jake drives to or from Revada ([Time](time.md#the-clock)). |
| **Starting position** | A hostel bunk, $15,000, and no vehicle. The inherited account is story context only and does not exist as a system. See [Backstory](backstory.md#the-money). |
| **Online** | Asynchronous only; fully playable offline. See [Blueprint Network](blueprint-network.md). |
