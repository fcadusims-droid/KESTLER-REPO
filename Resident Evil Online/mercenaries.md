# Mercenaries
*Score attack · PvE · 1 to 2 players*

A compact stage, a horde, and a clock in plain sight. It is the only mode that shows one, because here the clock is the point. The dead wound, but do not infect.

- **Score.** Every kill scores. Kills chained close together build a combo that multiplies every point that follows.
- **Time.** The clock runs down from the first second. Time Bonuses scattered through the stage add to it. Melee kills add a few seconds; finishing blows add more.
- **Characters.** Fixed loadouts only.
- **Ranks.** Every run ends in a rank, from C to SSS. Ranks unlock new characters and loadouts.
- **Partners.** A pair shares one clock and one score.

**Formats.** **Arena:** one stage, one clock. **Route:** several connected areas, the clock carried between them, with a supply point between areas where the run's earnings buy ammunition and the player chooses one of three abilities for the rest of the run.

**ΜΝΗΜΗ in Mercenaries.** Whenever the multiplier holds at its peak, she raises the stakes: heavier creatures, tighter spaces, denser waves, a Tyrant when the combo is at its highest. She builds stages from every part of the reconstruction. Once a week she seals one version of each stage, the same for everyone, and ranked runs are played against that sealed version.
