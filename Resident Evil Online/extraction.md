# The Extraction

The extraction is how a Survivor night ends. It is never one thing in one place. Every Survivor map holds several ways out, each shaped after a way someone, once, really got out.

## ΜΝΗΜΗ's Verdict

ΜΝΗΜΗ measures the completion of the session. She weighs four things: the public objectives completed; the ways out made ready, by power restored, puzzles solved, and routes opened; the roads finished and the doses used; and how many of the living remain against the seats she has built. There is no meter for it, and survivors are never told how close it is. When she judges that enough has been done, she raises the signal.

Two things bind the verdict.

- She does not raise the signal in the first hour, unless the dead have already brought the living down to no more than the seats she has built. Then she stops waiting. When survivors brought the night there by killing one another, she lets the hour run (`director.md`, She Plans Ahead). In the Valley of 2004, Saddler's death raises it at any minute (`cast-valley-2004.md`, The Island's End).
- She does not wait forever. Whatever has been done, the signal rises by the end of the night's ninetieth minute. Survivors call it the Late Signal, because nobody earned it: the ways out that no one made ready still ask everything they ask, and the Frenzy is no kinder.

She tells survivors when the end has come. She never tells them how near it was.

## The Ways Out

At Assembly, ΜΝΗΜΗ decides which of a map's ways out she will open tonight. She always opens at least two, far apart, and their seats fall within the session's range: no more than one per four survivors, no fewer than one per five. The ways she does not open stay dead all night: a train with no power, a helicopter that never comes.

Every way out is shaped after an escape from the records. Each keeps something of how that escape went, though not always the place where it happened.

Some ways out check who boards. Military and Umbrella-built ways out were made to keep the infected out, and they still do. Civilian ways out ask nothing of anyone.

## The Signal

```
ΜΝΗΜΗ: EXTRACTION SIGNAL ACTIVE.
WAYS OUT: 3.
```

A column of white light stands up over every way out she has opened. Over the ways that lie underground, it rises from the place where the road down to them begins: for the Emergency Train, the R.P.D. Each way out then makes its own sound: rotors in the distance, a train's horn under the street, an engine coughing on a lake.

She puts none of them on a map. There is the light, the sound, and the direction of both.

## The Frenzy

From the moment the signal is raised, the world goes into Frenzy. The dead converge on every way out and on every survivor moving toward one. Sound carries twice as far. Resonance roars, and every infected survivor lights up like a flare to everything that can sense the virus. Nothing stops coming.

## The Boarding

A way out carries anyone living who reaches it, until its seats are full. A companion or one of the Stranded takes a seat like anyone else, but never one a survivor within sight of the door could still take: the people of the records board last. Where a way out checks, it checks at the door, and whoever it turns back stays on the ground.

Seats are judged at departure, not at boarding. A survivor aboard whose Mark turns black before the way out leaves has crossed the Threshold in a seat, and the Turn comes twenty to forty seconds later, among everyone else aboard. Where a way out checks, the check acts again: the crew throws them out, the scanner opens the door and sounds, and the seat is open. Where it does not, the living aboard must decide, in the Last Breaths, whether to put them off, put them down, or stay beside them through the Turn. A Turned body cannot ride. If the Turn completes aboard, the thing it makes is thrown out at departure and the seat leaves empty. A Turned that kept its mind lands where the way out left it and goes on playing; one without a mind is Lost.

A survivor who leaves cured is **Extracted · Cured**. A survivor who leaves clean, never infected at all, is **Extracted · Clean**. A survivor who leaves still infected is **Extracted · Infected**, and what becomes of them is never told. The clock is paced so that a survivor bitten early will, in almost every session, turn long before the signal comes. A survivor bitten late may not, and every seat an infected survivor takes is a seat someone clean no longer has.

## The Last Call

Each way out waits for as long as ΜΝΗΜΗ decides; the Emergency Train waits only as long as its countdown runs. Every other way out leaves at once if every seat is filled. Otherwise it gives a Last Call before it leaves, about fifteen seconds long, in its own voice: a helicopter's rotors climbing in pitch and its cabin light turning from red to white, a train's horn, a boat's engine opening up, the cable car's bell, the gate's siren, the Army's trucks firing their engines. The Emergency Train's Last Call is the last fifteen seconds of the laboratory's countdown.

## The Twist

Once the signal is raised, ΜΝΗΜΗ may move one way out, once per session, and never without a cause survivors can see: a landing zone overrun, a rooftop collapsing, a fire spreading toward the pad, a tunnel caving in.

```
ΜΝΗΜΗ: EXTRACTION POINT RELOCATED.
REASON: LANDING ZONE COMPROMISED.
```

## The Climb

A way out that leaves is not yet out. Between leaving and being clear of the map, every way out that moves has a **Climb**, and while it climbs, what is still on the ground can reach it. Survivors aboard win only when it is clear (Departure, below).

| Way out | Its Climb | When it can be reached | By what |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Helicopters** | About thirty seconds, from lift-off to clear of the city | Its first ten seconds, while it is still low; the whole Climb, by what flies | Anything that leaps or climbs, from the ground or a roof; anything that flies |
| **Trains, and the cable car** | About forty-five seconds, from pulling out to the far tunnel or station | Its first fifteen seconds, onto the last car or the roof | Anything that leaps or climbs |
| **Trucks and transports** | About thirty seconds, from pulling away to past the wall or the barricade | Its first fifteen seconds, onto the back | Anything that leaps or climbs |
| **Boats** | From casting off to the end of the water | The whole Climb | Only what swims or flies |
| **The mine cart** | Its whole ride | The whole ride, in every gallery it passes | Whatever lives in the mines |

A way out on foot, such as the Breach, has no Climb: a survivor who is over is out. The Emergency Train's Climb is William Birkin's last car, and its beat decides it (`cast-raccoon-city-1998.md`). Every Climb ends before the missile or the island's end comes.

**What can leap.**

- **The Turned.** A Stalker, a Brute, or an Apex can leap onto a way out in its Climb. The Mass cannot. In the Valley, every Taken can. Leaping on to bring a way out down is not riding it (`the-turned.md`): a Turned never leaves the night by it.
- **ΜΝΗΜΗ's creatures.** She never sends one after a way out. A creature of hers that is already within reach when a way out leaves, brought there by the Frenzy and the noise like everything else, does what its kind does: what leaps, leaps, whoever is aboard (Second Law). Lickers, Hunters, Crimson Heads, Brood Hosts, Novistadors, and the named creatures whose records give them the reach, the Nemesis among them, can. The common dead cannot, and neither can the common hosts of the Valley, who want hosts, not bodies (`exclusions-and-the-plaga.md`).

**On board.** A creature that reaches a way out clings to it, to the skids, the roof, or the last car's rail, and tears at the machine. It can also strike anyone in its reach, as anywhere, and a survivor it pulls from a helicopter falls.

- **The count.** A creature that holds on for fifteen seconds brings the way out down. An Apex or a named creature needs eight. Each creature aboard keeps its own count, and the first to finish brings it down.
- **Fighting it off.** Everyone aboard can fight it with what they carry, and a shot aboard is Thunder, as anywhere. A creature put down or knocked loose falls, and its count is gone with it. Pilots never fight; crews do.
- **No Turn aboard.** A survivor whose Turn completes during the Climb is thrown out, as one whose Turn completes before departure is (The Boarding), and cannot leap back.

**The fall.** A way out brought down comes down within a few hundred meters of where it rose, along its road out, never beyond the edge of the map. A helicopter falls, a train or a truck is wrecked, a boat goes over. The wreck burns, and the fall is Thunder.

- **Who lives.** A way out brought down in the low part of its Climb leaves everyone aboard alive and down (`infection.md`). Brought down higher, it kills about half of those aboard where they sit. The wreck decides who, never ΜΝΗΜΗ. Companions and the Stranded aboard live or die the same way.
- **What falls with it.** The creature that brought it down falls too. A Turned is put down by the fall, and is Lost. An Apex and a named creature rise from the wreckage, as their records say they rise from everything.
- **After the fall.** A way out that falls has not left, and no one aboard it has won. Its seats are gone with it. Whoever lives through the crash is on the ground again, among the dead the fall is drawing, and must find another way out: one she opened that has not yet left, or, if none is left, the one more she opens when she judges it time, as she does when every way out leaves empty (Departure). In the Raccoon City of 1998 and the Valley of 2004, she opens one more only if it can still leave before the end. The end does not wait for anyone.

**Example: the Clock Tower.** A Solo night in the Raccoon City of 1998, after the signal.

| Step | What happens | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Four survivors board the Clock Tower helicopter: A and B clean, C cured, D with a fresh bite still in Seed. The helicopter lifts off. | Seats are judged at departure; a Mark in Seed passes. |
| 2 | E, whose survivor turned an hour ago in the park into a Swarm Host, mind still clear, has been waiting on the roof of the chapel. In the helicopter's first seconds, E leaps and catches a skid. | A Stalker can leap, while the helicopter is low. |
| 3 | A Hunter that the bell drew to the courtyard leaps too, and misses. | ΜΝΗΜΗ sent nothing. The Frenzy brought it, and it did what its kind does. |
| 4 | E tears at the tail. A fires out of the door, and the shot is Thunder over the whole park. E's claws pull B out of the door, and B falls. | Everyone aboard can fight; a creature can strike anyone in its reach. |
| 5 | E holds on for fifteen seconds. The helicopter goes down in Raccoon Park, two hundred meters from the tower. | The count is finished. |
| 6 | It fell from high. A dies in the wreck. C and D crawl out, down, with the park's dead coming toward the fire. E is put down by the fall, and is Lost. | About half of those aboard die; the wreck decides who. |
| 7 | C sprays her own wounds and raises D, and they run for the Checkpoint on the western highway, which has not yet left. D's Mark has no lines yet, and passes the soldiers. | A survivor who is down can heal only themselves; then they can raise another. The survivors of a crash must find another way out. |

In the Valley the same can happen to Mike's helicopter, to the Tower Helicopter, or to the cable car in the middle of the gorge, and the survivors of the fall have the rest of the Valley, and the island's count, between them and the next way out.

## Departure

When a way out is clear, every survivor aboard it wins. The session ends when the last way out she opened has cleared or fallen, and no survivor can still reach another. Survivors left behind do not win. The Turned still standing win if fewer than half of the survivors alive at the signal got out (`the-turned.md`, The Turned Win).

If every way out leaves with no survivor aboard, or falls, while living survivors remain somewhere in the world, the session does not end. The night goes on, and ΜΝΗΜΗ opens another way out when she judges it time. If no living survivor remains anywhere, the session ends there, and the Turned still standing win (`the-turned.md`, The Turned Win).

In the Raccoon City of 1998, the missile ends the night instead (`cast-raccoon-city-1998.md`, The Last Morning). In the Valley of 2004, the island's end does (`cast-valley-2004.md`, The Island's End).
