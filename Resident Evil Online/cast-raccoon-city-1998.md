# The People of Raccoon City, 1998

A night in the Raccoon City of 1998 runs the city's last three days, from the morning Jill Valentine escaped her apartment to the dawn the missile fell.

```
ΜΝΗΜΗ: RACCOON CITY. 28 SEPTEMBER 1998.
```

| Act | Day | Opens | Threads that begin |
|---|---|---|---|
| **I** | 28 September | At the waking | Jill Valentine, from her apartment in Uptown. The U.B.C.S.: Carlos Oliveira, Mikhail Viktor, Nikolai Zinoviev, Tyrell Patrick, Murphy Seeker. The Nemesis. |
| **II** | 29 September | At the thirtieth minute | Leon Kennedy and Claire Redfield, who arrive in a police cruiser down the Arklay road and crash in Downtown. Ada Wong. HUNK. The T-00. |

**Named creatures.** Three creatures of the records walk the city on threads of their own. Their beats release them, not a region's Crisis (`the-dead-and-engineered.md`). Each can be killed, and each takes longer to kill than any other Heavy. Put down, it rises a few minutes later in its next form, worse each time, as the records show, and only put down in its last form is it dead for the night (`survival.md`, The Heavy). The end the records gave it ends it at once, in any form. A beat that needs a creature already dead does not happen (`story-nights.md`, Story Nights).

**The Last Morning.** In the Raccoon City of 1998, the signal is the missile. ΜΝΗΜΗ raises it as always, by her verdict or at the ninetieth minute (`extraction.md`), and it opens Act III.

```
ΜΝΗΜΗ: 30 SEPTEMBER 1998.
A MISSILE IS ON ITS WAY TO RACCOON CITY.
TIME TO IMPACT: 20 MINUTES.
WAYS OUT: 3.
ELPIS: 2 DOSES REMAIN.
```

- **The count.** The strike comes twenty minutes after the alert, or ten seconds after the last living survivor has gone, whichever is sooner (`extraction.md`, The End of the Night). There is no clock on the screen, as there is none anywhere in a Survivor night. The count is in the world: ΜΝΗΜΗ's public voice gives the time left through every radio, siren, and loudspeaker at fifteen, ten, and five minutes, then every minute, then every second of the last ten. At ten minutes the Last Dose of Elpis opens, and she says so (`elpis-and-the-road.md`, The Reserve).
- **The ways out.** Every way out leaves no later than one minute before impact, full or not, after its Last Call. A way out that has not left by then never will.
- **No second chance.** The rule that the night goes on when every way out leaves empty (`extraction.md`) does not hold here. The strike ends the night. The one exception is a way out brought down in its Climb: if it was the last, ΜΝΗΜΗ opens one more, but only if it can still leave before impact (`extraction.md`, The Climb).
- **At impact.** Everything still in the city falls with it: survivors, Echoes, companions, the Stranded, the Turned, and the dead. A survivor in the city at impact has fallen, and the After-Action Report says how. The Turned go with the city: their race ended when they turned (`the-turned.md`, No Victory for the Turned).

**The named dead.** Whoever a beat kills, if the records say they rose, walks among the dead in their own clothes from then on: Brad Vickers in the R.P.D. District, Marvin Branagh in the main hall, Robert Kendo in his shop. A person whose beat never happens never walks.

Some of the named dead were dead before the night began. Will, who worked the bar at J's Bar and was the first in it to be bitten, walks outside it from the waking.

**Not in the city.** Chris Redfield is in Europe, and so is Barry Burton, except on the nights above. Sherry Birkin and every other child of the records are never placed: Emma Kendo, Lucia Rosso, and Lucy, the girl lost in the woods. Some people of the records are never placed in a Survivor night at all:

- **Outside the city.** Albert Lester, his wife Dorothy, and Regan were in the woods and the abandoned hospital in the hills, beyond the Army's barricades. They stay in *Flashback*, one of the eight's records.
- **Ended by what the night does not hold.** Dr. Carter, whose record ends with the Tyrant he woke in the laboratory, stays in *End of the Road*, one of the eight's records.
- **Already dead, and never risen.** Katherine Warren, the mayor's daughter, died at Brian Irons's hands before the night began. She lies where he left her.
- **Watchers.** The Masterminds watched their trials through cameras and never walked the city. ΜΝΗΜΗ took their place (`mnemosyne.md`). Annette Birkin and Nikolai Zinoviev, who sat behind those cameras too, are placed as the city's records hold them.
- **Nights that never happened.** Some records tell nights that never were: the one in which Umbrella's Wolfpack and the U.S. team called Echo Six fought through the city and Leon Kennedy could die in it, and the one in which four people the city killed, among them a U.S.S. soldier called Ghost and an officer named Daniel Cortini, lived. ΜΝΗΜΗ checks her nights against what happened, and these did not. No one from them is placed in a Survivor night.
