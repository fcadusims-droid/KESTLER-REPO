# The Session

## The Unit of Play

A **session** is a single, self-contained night of survival: a Survivor night. It has a map, a condition, a population, a set of threats, a supply of resources, an infection pace, an Elpis Reserve, ways out and seats, and a strain map, all assembled fresh by ΜΝΗΜΗ.

## Survivor at a Glance

| Mode | Kind | Survivors | You begin with |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Survivor** | Extraction survival horror · PvPvE | 40 to 100, by map, in Solo, Duo, Trio, or Squad of 4 | Nothing |

Survivor is the only mode of Resident Evil Online. Every rule in these documents, outside the `08-future-development` folder, describes it. The other modes once designed beside it are kept as possible future development (`future-development.md`). In the Valley of 2004, the Plaga replaces the virus, Elpis, and the strains (`exclusions-and-the-plaga.md`).

## The Lifecycle of a Survivor Night

1. **Request.** A sleeper asks ΜΝΗΜΗ for a night, alone or with their squad, in the classic rotation or in the Ruins (`worlds.md`, Raccoon City · Ruins, 2026).
2. **Assembly.** ΜΝΗΜΗ selects the map, its version, and, for a story night, its threads and beats, and builds the session: condition (the hour, the weather, and the state of the city), strain map, Elpis Reserve, ways out and seats, threats, resources, companions, the Stranded, puzzles. Then she places every subject at a waking point.
3. **The Waking.** Each survivor wakes where ΜΝΗΜΗ put them, clean, alone, and empty-handed.
4. **Observation.** ΜΝΗΜΗ watches everything from the first footstep. Survivors choose: cooperate, separate, hide, hunt, betray.
5. **Infection.** The first survivors are bitten, scratched, or needled. Their Marks rise and their clocks start.
6. **Trials.** Each infected survivor's road to the cure takes shape.
7. **Disturbance.** Events begin to surface.
8. **Elpis.** Doses are earned, seen, fought over, and taken.
9. **The Turn.** Infected survivors who run out of time turn.
10. **The Signal.** ΜΝΗΜΗ judges that enough has been done, or the night runs out of time, and opens the ways out.
11. **Extraction.** Survivors reach a way out before it leaves, or do not.
12. **Debrief.** The After-Action Report is written.
13. **Closing.** ΜΝΗΜΗ closes the night. What each survivor lived goes to her, and its record to the Ledger. The sleeper keeps nothing, and goes back to the Waiting Dark (`sleepers-and-the-remembered.md`). The player keeps all of it.

## The Waking Points

There is no cabin, no drop, no jump to choose. Before the simulation starts, while every subject is still unconscious, ΜΝΗΜΗ places each one somewhere in the world. A survivor may wake in a bed in a third-floor apartment, on a rooftop above Downtown, in a stalled subway car, under a desk in a dentist's office, in the pew of a village church. Nobody remembers being placed. Everybody knows they were.

ΜΝΗΜΗ chooses each waking point from hundreds built into every map, at random, within one rule: **equal ground.** No survivor begins with an advantage that came from where they were put.

- **Clean and empty.** Every waking point is sheltered and free of the dead at the moment of waking. Nothing within reach is better than an improvised weapon: a pipe, a bat, a kitchen knife. No firearm, ammunition, or medicine lies in the room.
- **The same distance from everything that matters.** No waking point is near an armory, a vault, a laboratory, a safe room, a way out, a companion, or one of the Stranded. Each lies about as far from its nearest one as every other point does from theirs.
- **The same first danger.** Every waking point starts in a region at Low threat, with the same density of the dead around it.
- **Never shared.** No two survivors from different squads wake within sight or earshot of each other.
- **Squads together, but not in one room.** Members of a squad wake in the same building or on the same block, each in a different room, close enough to find one another by sound.
- **Never twice.** A survivor never wakes at the same point in two consecutive sessions.

**Empty Hands.** No survivor begins with anything: no weapon, no pack, no ration, no tool, no map, no compass, no marker. The one exception is the earpiece beside every waking point (`director.md`), which carries ΜΝΗΜΗ's voice and nothing else, and cannot be dropped, traded, or taken. Whatever a survivor carries from this moment on, they took from the world, and a survivor learns the world by its landmarks, or finds a map somewhere inside it.

## Scale

Every Survivor night is played on an **open world**: a whole place rebuilt as the records hold it, at Slow Burn. Its character is slow dread, long silences, and sudden collapse. A hundred survivors disappear into a whole city, and for long stretches each one feels alone.

No Survivor night announces its length. It ends when ΜΝΗΜΗ decides it has asked enough of its survivors and the last way out has left, and it rarely ends in less than an hour.

## The Shape of a Survivor Night

Before the first survivor wakes, ΜΝΗΜΗ fixes three numbers. They decide what kind of night it will be, and none of them is ever shown.

**The Reserve.** One dose of Elpis for every ten survivors, rounded down, and never fewer than four. A Survivor night of sixty-four holds six doses. A Survivor night of a hundred holds ten.

**The Seats.** The ways out ΜΝΗΜΗ opens hold, together, no more than one seat for every four survivors and no fewer than one for every five, both rounded down. A Survivor night of sixty-four has between twelve and sixteen seats. A Survivor night of a hundred has between twenty and twenty-five. Every map is built so that some combination of at least two of its ways out fits that range for every population it can hold.

**The Clock.** No one carries the virus when they wake. A survivor's clock starts the moment they are infected, and from then on the virus is paced so that a body never wounded again and never cured reaches the Threshold in about forty minutes. Wounds, appetites, and ΜΝΗΜΗ's Surge make it sooner. Nothing a survivor does turns it back. An antiviral found in the world can hold it still for a few minutes (`infection.md`), a safe room holds it while a survivor shelters there (`survival.md`, Safe Rooms), and one strain slows itself: a T-Phobos carrier who keeps calm burns at half pace.

Put together, those numbers mean one thing. Every survivor enters the night clean, and most will not stay that way. Six can be cured. Sixteen at most can leave. Every survivor who is infected and not cured has about forty minutes from the wound, and the night is longer than that. An infected survivor who reaches a seat takes it from someone who might have been clean.

If the night goes badly enough, the Turned who kept their minds come to outnumber the living. When that moment comes, ΜΝΗΜΗ announces it.

```
ΜΝΗΜΗ: THE DEAD OUTNUMBER THE LIVING.
```

Survivors call what follows the Second Night.

## The Session Blueprint

ΜΝΗΜΗ assembles every session from layers. Every layer but the last is seeded before the survivors arrive and rewritten as the session unfolds. The last is written by the survivors themselves.

**FORMAT** + **MAP** + **VERSION** + **THREADS AND BEATS** + **CONDITION** + **WAKING POINTS** + **STRAIN MAP** + **INFECTION PACE** + **THREATS** + **RESOURCES** + **ELPIS RESERVE** + **WAYS OUT AND SEATS** + **COMPANIONS** + **THE STRANDED** + **PUZZLES** + **OBJECTIVES** + **RESTRICTIONS** + **EVENTS** + **SURVIVOR BEHAVIOR**
