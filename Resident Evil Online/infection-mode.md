# Infection
*Monster hunt · PvP · 5 players: four sweepers and one monster · 8 to 16 in Free-for-All and Hive*

One monster against the people sent to kill it. The monster begins as a generic zombie, weak, slow, and easy to kill, and the match is about what it becomes before the sweepers find it. The sweepers arrive armed, as people of the records, and the match is about finding it before it becomes too much to kill.

## Choose Your Virus

| Virus | Strength | Weakness | Path |
|---|---|---|---|
| **T-Virus** | Brute force and pursuit | Loud, slow to evolve | Zombie → Crimson Head → Tyrant-class → **Nemesis-class**, with the parasite |
| **G-Virus** | Regeneration; grows with every victim | Unstable when overfed | Zombie → Brood Host → G-Mutant → **G-form** |
| **C-Virus** | Shapeshifting and surprise | Mutations can misfire | Zombie → J'avo → Chrysalid → **Haos-class** |
| **Uroboros** | Speed and reach | Starves if it stops feeding; burns | Zombie → Mkono-class → Revenant-class → **Aheri-class** |
| **T-Phobos** | Explosive transformation when provoked | Weak until it is | Zombie → Afflicted → Sploder → **Berserker-class Afflicted** |
| **T-Veronica** | Keeps its wits; commands lesser creatures | Fragile alone | Zombie → Swarm Host → Nosferatu-class → **Queen-form** |

Infection is a trial, not a night of the war, and the rules of minds do not apply: every monster here is played from its first form to its last. ΜΝΗΜΗ runs it to learn what each virus does when people hunt it, and what it does to another, not what happens to a person.

## The Monster

- **First in.** The monster wakes alone somewhere on the map. The sweepers arrive by helicopter a minute later.
- **Feeding.** It feeds on the Scattered (Evolution, below), on corpses, and on sweepers. Every feeding grows it toward its next form.
- **Body and growth.** Every monster has a body and the growth around it. Harm takes the growth first. Feeding regrows the growth, never the body. Only a new form makes the body whole again.
- **The change.** To take its next form, the monster stops where it stands and changes. The change takes ten seconds, it is Thunder (`the-dead-and-engineered.md`), and while it lasts the monster takes double harm. Sweepers who catch it changing can end the match there.
- **Scent.** It can stop and draw breath: for a few seconds, every living thing near it shows to it through walls.
- **Creeping.** It can move slowly and quietly. Creeping, it leaves no crows behind and makes no sound above a Murmur.

## The Sweepers

Four players, each a person of the records in one of four roles. Each carries the setup that person carries in Versus, and their role's gear, which is the same for everyone in that role and is not counted in the setup's 100.

| Role | Who can take it | Their gear | What they are for |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Assault** | Chris Redfield, Carlos Oliveira, Jack Krauser | A ballistic shield: raised, it halves the harm they take for a few seconds, then must recover | Doing the most harm, and standing in front |
| **Tracker** | Jill Valentine, HUNK, Ada Wong | A motion tracker that shows movement nearby for a moment; and the lockdown, which drops the containment shutters around a single block for a minute, so that nothing gets out of it and nothing gets in | Finding the monster, and keeping it where the others can reach it |
| **Medic** | Rebecca Chambers, Sheva Alomar | A dart gun that heals a sweeper at range; helps up a fallen sweeper in half the time | Keeping the others standing |
| **Support** | Leon S. Kennedy, Claire Redfield, Albert Wesker | Flares that light the monster for every sweeper for a few seconds; once in each life, a radio call that brings the helicopter's gun down on a lit monster | Showing the others where to shoot, and covering them |

- **One of each.** A team of sweepers has one of each role.
- **Down, and back.** A sweeper brought down can be helped up by another. A sweeper who dies comes back on the helicopter's next run, which comes every two minutes.
- **Reading the trail.** Nothing is marked. Crows rise from wherever the monster passes, unless it creeps. The Scattered scream when it takes them, and a scream is a Clamor. A wounded monster bleeds where it walks, and fresh corpses show where it fed.

## Winning the Hunt

- **The sweepers win** when the monster dies. In the Hunt, it dies once.
- **The monster wins** when all four sweepers are dead at the same moment; or when, at its last form, it destroys the containment generator, the one machine on the map that keeps the zone sealed, and gets out; or when the match's twenty minutes end with it still alive. There is no clock on the screen: ΜΝΗΜΗ's public voice gives the last five minutes, the last minute, and the last ten seconds.
- **No hiding.** A monster that neither feeds nor fights for two minutes draws the crows: they rise over it wherever it is, creeping or not.

## Evolution

Monsters feed on **the Scattered**: armed survivors placed through the map before the match begins, built as Echoes from the sleepers of Versus and Mercenaries. Until those Echoes exist, ΜΝΗΜΗ plays the Scattered herself, as she plays the empty places of an Outbreak. They shoot back. Monsters also feed on corpses, on sweepers, and, in Free-for-All and Hive, on each other. A kill feeds more than a corpse, and a sweeper or another monster feeds most of all. Every form needs more feeding than the last. A monster at its last form is the hardest thing in the match to kill, and in Free-for-All every monster behind it is hunting it. It also burns: a last form loses strength every moment it is not feeding.

Each time a T-Virus monster becomes a Tyrant, ΜΝΗΜΗ drops a parasite case near it, without light. The Tyrant must reach it before a sweeper or another monster destroys it. If it is destroyed, that Tyrant stays a Tyrant.

## Formats

- **The Hunt.** Four sweepers, one monster, as above.
- **Rescue.** The Scattered are people to be saved, not fed on: they are unarmed and hide where they woke. The sweepers win if five of them reach the helicopter; the monster wins if it takes enough of them that five never can.
- **Brood.** The monster wakes beside six brood sacs of its virus. The sweepers win by destroying all six. The monster can hatch one into a lesser creature of its own virus that fights beside it, and wins as in the Hunt.
- **Defend.** The monster begins at its last form, with lesser creatures of its virus. The sweepers hold two generators and the helicopter's landing pad until the helicopter comes. The monster wins by destroying them.
- **Evacuation.** Five matches in a row, on five maps, with the same five players. The side that wins a match takes an advantage into the next: a sweepers' win leaves the Army's turrets standing on the next map; a monster's win leaves the next map's Scattered fewer and its own kind more. The fifth match is always Defend.
- **Free-for-All and Hive.** Eight to sixteen players, every one of them a monster, and no sweepers. In Free-for-All the last monster standing wins; in Hive, players who chose the same virus fight as one faction. A monster put down here rises once more as a generic zombie and climbs again, and the second time it is put down, it is gone. If the field stagnates, ΜΝΗΜΗ releases a catalyst that pushes every monster a stage forward and herds them together.
