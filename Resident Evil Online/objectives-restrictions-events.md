# Objectives, Restrictions, and Events

## The Primary Objective

Every Survivor night rests on one purpose.

# SURVIVE UNTIL EXTRACTION.

Survive the virus. Survive the dead. Survive each other. The virus does not wait, and ΜΝΗΜΗ does not let the quiet become a refuge, which is why she creates tasks that draw survivors out.

## Public Objectives

Most objectives are announced in ΜΝΗΜΗ's public voice, to anyone who can hear, and they are the work of the clean as much as the marked. A clean survivor has no road and hears no private voice, but every public objective they complete brings the extraction closer, opens routes, and unseals caches. A clean survivor's night is spent staying clean, finding the ways out before the signal, and doing the work that makes the signal come.

| Objective | Description |
|---|---|
| **LOCATE** | Find a specific item |
| **ACTIVATE** | Restore power or systems in a region |
| **RECOVER** | Retrieve a vital resource |
| **RESCUE** | Find someone who needs help: a companion or one of the Stranded |
| **ESCAPE** | Reach a specific point |
| **DEFEND** | Protect a location for a set time |
| **INVESTIGATE** | Trace the source of a signal |
| **TRANSPORT** | Carry something across the map |
| **ENDURE** | Remain inside a dangerous zone for a set time |
| **ARMS** | Do any of the above for a different prize: where one of the night's secret weapons lies (Secret Weapons, below) |

The first public objectives of a Survivor night are announced within its first five minutes, at least one in or beside every region where survivors woke, so that no one begins a night with nothing to do but walk. Objectives appear, change, and disappear. Each one feeds the session's completion, and many of them are also Missions on someone's road. ΜΝΗΜΗ's objectives never lie, but they never promise safety.

In Survivor, whoever completes a public objective learns something no one else does. Every objective is finished at something that keeps a record, a terminal, a radio, a switchboard, a ledger on a desk, and the moment it is finished that record shows one of the ways out ΜΝΗΜΗ has built for tonight and whether she will open it. It is written in the world, not spoken: a clean survivor can read it, and so can whoever is standing beside them. Before the light rises, doing the night's work is the only way to know where the night ends.

## Restrictions

ΜΝΗΜΗ imposes temporary rules that force survivors to rethink everything. Every restriction comes with its reason.

```
THE HOSPITAL WING IS BEING SEALED. REASON: CONTAINMENT BREACH.
POWER SUPPLY HAS BEEN INTERRUPTED. REASON: SUBSTATION FAILURE.
THE UNDERGROUND LEVEL IS NOW CLASSIFIED: EXTREME THREAT. REASON: B.O.W. RELEASED.
ALL VEHICLES IN THIS DISTRICT ARE DISABLED. REASON: FUEL CONTAMINATION.
MEDICAL SUPPLIES CANNOT BE FOUND IN THIS DISTRICT. REASON: PHARMACIES LOOTED.
THE NORTH DISTRICT HAS BEEN RECLASSIFIED: HIGH THREAT. REASON: HUNTERS SIGHTED.
```

Restrictions do not just add difficulty. They invalidate plans.

## Puzzles

The places ΜΝΗΜΗ rebuilds were full of locks never meant for keys alone: statues that open passages, medallions that fit only one pedestal, panels switched in the right order, dials hidden behind paintings. She rebuilds them all. In a Survivor night, the R.P.D. is complete, from the lobby to the underground, and on any night its puzzle is live, its way down opens only for whoever brings the three medallions to the goddess in the main hall. The castle's halls still answer to their emblems, their swords, and their clocks.

Puzzles do three things.

- **They open the map.** Some regions, and the shortest routes between others, lie behind a puzzle.
- **They open the ways out.** Several ways out ask for a puzzle before they will run.
- **They guard the vaults.**

**No puzzle can be learned by heart.** ΜΝΗΜΗ keeps the shape of every puzzle exactly as the records hold it and changes its answer every session. The clue to every answer is always somewhere in the world: in a memo, a painting, a diary, a scratch on a wall.

**Not every lock, every night.** At Assembly ΜΝΗΜΗ decides which of a map's puzzles are live tonight, usually about half. The rest are found as the night left them: forced open by the dead, jammed, or behind a collapse, and a survivor who knows the map has to find out which before they plan around it. A vault's puzzle is always live. A puzzle that stands on the only road to a way out she has opened is never sealed: it is live, or it is open.

**Some puzzles need more than one pair of hands.** Two levers on opposite sides of a hall pulled together. A pressure plate that holds a door only while someone stands on it, on the far side. A code read aloud in one room and typed in another. These were built for two people who trusted each other, and in a Survivor night the two are often strangers. A Solo survivor who finds one can find a companion, find a stranger and talk, or walk away.

## Vaults

Behind the hardest puzzles lie **vaults**: a gun shop's back room, the R.P.D.'s armory, a castle treasury, a sealed laboratory locker. A vault holds what nothing else in the session holds: a magnum and rounds for it, a grenade launcher, a single rocket, body armor, a full map of the city, keys to every safe room in a district. There is no glow to tell a survivor how good a thing is.

A vault behind a lock built for two always holds enough for two, and when the two are strangers it is the most honest test of trust in the game.

## Secret Weapons

Every map holds a few weapons beyond anything a vault keeps: the ones the people of the records used against the worst things in their nights. They are the city's rarest finds and the strongest answer a group has to the Heavy: with one, two survivors can put down what otherwise takes a crowd (`survival.md`, Run First). They are never a way to fight one alone.

### The Rules

- **A few each night.** At Assembly, ΜΝΗΜΗ places two to four of the map's secret weapons, each once. No night holds the whole list, and no two nights need hold the same ones.
- **Hidden behind the hardest ways in.** Each lies in one of the places listed for it below: where the records left it, or where a weapon of its kind would be kept. ΜΝΗΜΗ chooses the place at Assembly, so knowing the records is never enough to know where it is tonight. Every place is behind something: a puzzle chain, a lock built for two, a flooded passage, a region at High threat, a horde. None is near a waking point, and none glows.
- **Finite.** Each comes with its own ammunition and no more: no round, shell, or tank found anywhere else in the night fits it. When it is empty, it is a heavy thing to carry. The records' versions with endless ammunition do not exist in ΜΝΗΜΗ's world.
- **Some are carried by companions.** A companion whose records put one of the map's secret weapons in their hands may wake carrying it, if ΜΝΗΜΗ chooses so at Assembly. It is one of the night's two to four, and it lies nowhere else that night. It is the companion's alone: they fire it themselves, never hand it over, and no one can take it from them. If they die or turn, it lies spent and broken where they fell, and no one carries it away (`survivor-mode.md`, Companions).
- **Carried by survivors like anything else.** A long one goes on the shoulders, a handgun in a holster or a pocket, and the heaviest fill both hands (`survival.md`, The Inventory). Everyone can see what a survivor carries. At the Turn it falls with everything else, plain, for anyone to take (`cosmetics-and-dossier.md`).
- **The ARMS objective.** Sometimes ΜΝΗΜΗ announces a public objective whose prize is a secret weapon's place. Like any public objective, it is open to everyone, and the first to finish it reads the prize on the record where it ends (Public Objectives, above). That survivor learns where the weapon is. They are not given it. Whoever stands beside them reads it too, whoever follows them finds it, and whoever kills them on the way can take whatever they found. ΜΝΗΜΗ never says who finished first.

```
ΜΝΗΜΗ: A WEAPON OF THE RECORDS IS IN THIS CITY.
WHOEVER RESTORES THE SUBSTATION WILL KNOW WHERE.
```

- **In the Night.** ΜΝΗΜΗ places them because the records do, and because she wants to know what strangers do when one of them is holding the surest thing in the city against a Tyrant.

## Events

**BLACKOUT.** A region loses power. Lights die, security fails, and the dark takes over.

**OVERRUN.** A district floods with infected, always after a sign: a siren, a breached barricade, a scream.

**LOCKDOWN.** Doors seal. Exits close. Open paths become cages.

**EMERGENCY.** A broadcast announces a new crisis.

**B.O.W. RELEASE.** A catastrophic creature breaks out of containment, and its region becomes Extreme.

**SUPPLY DROP.** Valuable resources fall into a dangerous place.

**ELPIS DROP.** A dose falls under an amber strobe.

**STRAIN DROP.** A pure strain falls under a red strobe. Never in the Valley.

**PARASITE DROP.** A parasite falls without light. Never in the Raccoon City of 1998 or the Valley (`strains.md`).

**EXTRACTION SIGNAL.** Columns of white light over every way out.

**COLLAPSE.** Part of the map is destroyed or sealed. Routes vanish.

**CONVERGENCE.** A signal, a beacon, a fire at the heart of the map that pulls the living toward each other.

**UNKNOWN EVENT.** Something no survivor has seen before. She announces that it has begun. The world shows what it is.

```
ΜΝΗΜΗ: UNKNOWN EVENT INITIATED.
```
