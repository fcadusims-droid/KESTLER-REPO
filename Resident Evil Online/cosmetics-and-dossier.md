# Cosmetics and the Dossier

## Cosmetics

### The Rule

Everything sold or earned in Resident Evil Online changes how something looks and nothing else. Because Survivor is read by eye and by ear, "nothing else" is held to precise limits:

- **Shape and tier.** A cosmetic keeps the silhouette of what it dresses. A light helmet still looks like a light helmet and a heavy one like a heavy one; a pistol still looks like a pistol.
- **Brightness.** Nothing is darker, duller, or better hidden against a wall or in the dark than the plain item it replaces. A dress or gear piece that blends into the night is never made.
- **No light, no sound.** No cosmetic glows, flickers, or makes a sound of its own. Footsteps are footsteps.
- **Every dress covers the same skin.** Cloth hides a Mark (`infection.md`, The Mark), so what cloth covers is fixed and the same in every look: the body, the arms to the wrists, and the legs to the ankles. No dress, gear, or charm covers the hands, the neck, the jaw, or the lower face, where the lines reach at Fracture, or the eyes, where a Plaga shows in the Valley. Every wound tears and stains a dress the same way. No look makes anyone harder to read than another.
- **Hit areas are the body's.** No cosmetic changes the size of a survivor, a Turned, or a monster, or where it can be hurt.
- **Forms keep their strain.** A monster's look keeps its strain's colors and patterns and every weak point the strain gives it: the glowing core of Uroboros, the yellow eye-tumors of the G-Virus.
- **Gestures stay shared.** The raised hand, the lowered weapon, and the signal to follow are the same for everyone, because survivors use them to talk. Poses bought or earned are for the Dossier.
- **The tone holds.** Everything worn could be worn by someone in these places. Event dress may be macabre; it is never a joke.

Companions and the Stranded are never dressed: Leon Kennedy in a Survivor night is who the records say he was. Every original survivor can be dressed.

### What Can Be Owned

| **Figurines** | The small collectibles the series has always hidden in its rooms: Mr. Raccoon, the Mr. Raccoon Memoriams of the ruins, Mr. Charlie, Mr. Everywhere, the Goats of Warding, the Clockwork Castellans | The shelf of the Dossier (The Dossier, in this document) |

### Gear in Survivor

In Survivor, a survivor wakes with nothing but their dress. Everything else they wear, they find (`survival.md`). A player's gear looks appear on what they find.

- **Putting it on turns it on.** A player who has dressed their survivor in a BSAA set wakes in its dress. When they find a riot helmet and put it on, it becomes the BSAA helmet, in the riot helmet's shape. When they find a police vest and put it on, it becomes the BSAA vest. A bag becomes the BSAA pack.
- **Everyone sees it.** Every survivor sees every other survivor in the looks that survivor's player chose. A player sees others in theirs, and is seen in their own.
- **The look belongs to the player, not the item.** A survivor who takes off the helmet and drops it drops a plain riot helmet. Whoever picks it up and puts it on wears it in their own helmet look if they own one, and as a plain riot helmet if they do not.
- **A set is made for every tier.** A gear look is drawn once for each tier of its slot, so a BSAA helmet on a hard hat and a BSAA helmet on a riot helmet still read as light and heavy.
- **Finishes and charms follow the hands.** A found handgun takes the player's handgun finish when they pick it up, and loses it when it leaves their hands. Their charm hangs from whatever bag or weapon they carry.
- **At the Turn, everything carried falls.** Gear and weapons drop where the survivor fell, plain again, for anyone to take; the one exception is what an Afflicted keeps in its hand (`the-turned.md`). The body rises in the player's Form for its strain and shape, and a shape that keeps a human outline, like the zombie, keeps the dress it died in. A squad that meets a friend at the bottom of a stairwell an hour later may meet them in that dress.

In the Night, none of this is strange. ΜΝΗΜΗ composes every body she runs from the Remembered, and a helmet is only a helmet to her. What it looks like on a sleeper is what the sleeper asked her to show.

### Echoes in the Wardrobe

Echoes wear dress, gear, and Forms drawn from the Wardrobe as often as players were seen wearing them, and never a trophy (Trophies, in this document), so that nothing on an Echo tells a survivor that it is one. A shared Echo wears its owner's looks.

## The Dossier

Every player has a **Dossier**: a personnel file in the style the series has always kept, with a photograph clipped to its cover. The photograph is the **Portrait**, a small scene the player builds and the first thing friends see.

### The Portrait

A Portrait is a three-dimensional scene, built and saved like a photograph.

- **The subject.** The player's original survivor in any dress and gear they own, or one of their Forms.
- **The pose.** Standing, crouched, leaning against a concrete wall, sitting on the hood of a wrecked cruiser, holding a weapon in its finish.
- **The camera.** Angle, distance, height, tilt, and lens, set freely, as long as the subject's face stays in the frame.
- **The backdrop.** A place from the maps: the R.P.D. lobby, the clock tower in the rain, the subway platform, the crater's edge, the village plaza, a castle hall. Or the player's own diorama (The Diorama, below).
- **The light.** Any condition the place allows: day, night, rain, or blackout.
- **The frame.** The folder the photograph is clipped to: an Umbrella personnel file, an R.P.D. case file, a S.T.A.R.S. roster card, a BSAA dossier.
- **The shelf.** Up to five figurines along the bottom of the file.
- **The plaques.** Up to three lines from the player's own Ledger: nights survived, clean extractions, cures given away, the Stranded brought out.
- **The emblems.** Up to three, pinned to the cover.
- **The marks.** The First Run plaque and the Patron mark, for those who hold them.
- **The standing seal.** In the top corner of every Dossier sits a small seal that shows the player's doorstep standing (`survival.md`, The Doorstep Record). It cannot be hidden, moved, covered, or bought away, and it changes only when the record does. Each seal has its own shape as well as its own color, so it reads for everyone (`presentation-and-technology.md`, Readable by Everyone):

| Standing | Seal |
|---|---|
| **Peaceful** | A white circle |
| **Warned** | A yellow triangle |
| **Notorious** | An orange square, with the word **Griefer** beside it |
| **Infamous** | A red cross, with the word **Griefer** beside it |

The seal speaks only of the doorstep. Betrayal inside a night, a lie, a stolen dose, a shot in the back on an open street, is part of Survivor (`the-living.md`, Betrayal) and never touches it. A Peaceful seal says a player keeps the safe rooms' peace, not that they can be trusted.

A player who wants their original survivor leaning against a concrete wall in the Ruins at night, in Umbrella dress, a Clockwork Castellan on the shelf and their count of cures given away under the photograph, builds exactly that.

### The Diorama

Every player also has a **diorama**: a room of their own, built piece by piece, kept behind the Portrait. The Portrait is a photograph; the diorama is the place it was taken. Anyone who can open a player's Dossier can step into their diorama and walk around it.

- **The space.** One room, up to about twelve meters by twelve, and five high, on one or two levels. A player builds it from nothing, or starts from a kit and changes it.
- **The pieces.** Walls, floors, and ceilings in the materials of the records: R.P.D. wood panelling, Umbrella white tile, mansion stone, village plaster, castle masonry, the concrete of ARK. Doors, from the R.P.D.'s carved emblem doors to a laboratory airlock. Windows, stairs, railings, and rubble. Furniture and props: desks, specimen tanks, an item box, a typewriter with an ink ribbon beside it, filing cabinets, a gun rack, herbs in pots, a church pew, an operating table.
- **The light and the weather.** Lamps and emergency lights, each placed and aimed. The hour and the weather outside the windows, from any the maps allow (`worlds.md`, Time and Weather).
- **The models.** Up to twelve in a room: the player's own survivor in any dress and gear they own, their Forms, and models of the people and creatures of the records. Each takes a pose from its own list, and every pose is one the records or the game already use: Leon Kennedy aiming down a corridor, Ada Wong at a window, a Licker on the ceiling, a Tyrant halfway through a door.
- **The limits.** A room holds up to three hundred pieces. Nothing can be written freely: signs, labels, and screens show only the lines the shop sells. Models cannot be placed in contact poses outside the ones their lists hold. The tone holds here as everywhere (The Rule, in this document).
- **More than one.** A player can keep up to five dioramas and choose which one stands behind the Portrait.

**Building a scene.** A player who wants an Umbrella laboratory starts from the laboratory kit: white tile, a specimen tank with something in it, a sealed airlock, a red emergency light turning. They place their own survivor at the console in a lab coat, William Birkin behind the glass, a Hunter in the tank, and HUNK in the doorway, rifle up. They set the hour to night and the light to blackout, take the Portrait from the airlock window, and every friend who opens their Dossier can walk in.

**Where the pieces come from.** Most diorama pieces and kits are in the Wardrobe, for Ribbons or money, at the prices of `economy.md`. The models of the Heavy and of the named are unlocked only by deeds in the nights, and some pieces and models are trophies that can never be bought (Four Ways to Own, below).

**One day, a place to stand.** A possible future feature, not scheduled, would let a player walk into a diorama as their survivor and use it in place of the menus between nights (`the-refuge.md`).

**In the Night.** A diorama is a room ΜΝΗΜΗ lets a sleeper arrange from the Archive: every wall is a photograph she keeps, every model a record she has rebuilt. She watches what they put in it. It is one more way of learning them.

### Four Ways to Own

Not everything that can be worn or displayed can be bought. Some things in Resident Evil Online can only be lived, and those are the things players show off.

| Way | How it is owned | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **Sold** | In the Wardrobe from the day it is offered, for Ribbons or money | Most dress, gear, finishes, charms, emblems, figurines, diorama pieces, and kits |
| **Earned or sold** | Given free by a deed in the nights, and in the Wardrobe for anyone who would rather not wait | Backdrops and many frames: extracting by the Clock Tower earns the clock tower, by the Subway the subway platform, and a clean extraction from the Ruins the crater's edge |
| **Unlocked** | Not in a player's Wardrobe at all until they have done the deed. After it, it appears there, for Ribbons or money | The models of the Heavy and of the named: putting down a Tyrant unlocks the Tyrant, living through a Nemesis that hunted you unlocks the Nemesis, bringing Leon Kennedy out of a night alive unlocks Leon, and putting down Saddler, or being there when he fell, unlocks Saddler |
| **Trophies** | Given only by the deed. Never sold, never in the Wardrobe, never gifted | Below |

### Trophies

A **trophy** is proof. It is a look, a model, a frame, an emblem, or a diorama piece that exists only because its owner did something hard, and it can be had no other way.

- **Earned only in the nights.** A trophy is given the moment its deed is done, in an online night among real sleepers. Echoes can be part of the night, but a deed done alone in an offline night earns no trophy, unless the trophy says it can.
- **Never for sale, never a gift.** No trophy is ever in the Wardrobe, at any price, in Ribbons or money, and none can be given to a friend.
- **It carries its night.** Every trophy shows when and where it was earned: the date, the map, and the night's line from the After-Action Report. And it shows how rare it is: the share of all players who hold it.
- **Never on an Echo.** Echoes never wear or carry trophies (Echoes in the Wardrobe, above), so a trophy seen on a Dossier is always a player's own.
- **Obtainable while its deed is.** A trophy stays earnable for as long as the deed can still be done (Promise 3). A trophy of an event is earnable whenever that event returns.

| Trophy | Its deed |
|---|---|
| **The railgun's mark**, an emblem | Be one of the survivors at the railgun when the Nemesis of the records falls to it (`cast-raccoon-city-1998.md`) |
| **Heavy Hunter**, a frame | Put down a Heavy that is not named with no more than three survivors, companions included, and no secret weapon (`survival.md`, Run First) |
| **The long way home**, a dress | Extract clean from five nights in a row |
| **Nobody's hero**, a figurine | Extract after giving away the only dose you ever held |
| **The keeper of the peace**, a pose | Keep a Peaceful seal through two hundred nights (`survival.md`, The Doorstep Record) |
| **The last seat**, a diorama piece: the Clock Tower helicopter's door | Take the last seat on the last way out of a Raccoon City night with less than two minutes to impact |
| **The Merchant's best customer**, a hat | In one night in the Valley, buy from every one of the Merchant's appearances |

**Secret trophies.** Some trophies are never listed. Their deeds are not published anywhere, in the game or by Capcom, and the first sign most players see of one is someone else's Dossier. A few are among the rarest things in the game: a model that appears once in many thousands of nights, a piece earned only by a deed few will think to try. Players will find them, share what they found, and argue about the rest. That is the point.

**In the Night.** A trophy is ΜΝΗΜΗ's remembering on a sleeper's behalf: a mark in their file of something they did and will never recall. She gives it so that the hand that plays them can show what the sleeper cannot.

### Where It Is Seen

The Dossier is shown on the friends list, to every member of a squad as it forms in the Waiting Dark, in a night's lobby (below), on every page of the Ledger, and on the screen that closes a night, for the survivors who left on the last way out. It is never shown inside a night: Survivor has no name tags, and no Portrait.

**In the lobby.** Before every Survivor night, while it is assembled, the lobby lists every player who will wake in it, each by a card: their name, their standing seal, and their First Run and Patron marks. The card never shows the Portrait, the dress, or anything else of how their survivor looks, so no one can match a card to a body once the night begins: the strangers stay strangers, and only their reputation at the door goes ahead of them. Echoes are never listed. A player who does not like the company can leave the lobby before the night begins and go back to the queue, at no cost but the wait.
