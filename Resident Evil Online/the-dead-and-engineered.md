# The Dead and the Engineered

## How the Dead Find You

The infected hunt by sound before they hunt by sight. Noise is the currency of danger, and every action carries a price.

| Sound Tier | Examples | Result |
|---|---|---|
| **Hush** | Crouched movement, careful searching, whispering | Almost undetectable |
| **Murmur** | Walking, opening a door slowly, ordinary speech | Nearby creatures may notice |
| **Clamor** | Running, melee strikes, forcing a lock, breaking glass, shouting | Draws the dead from several streets away |
| **Thunder** | Gunfire, explosions, engines, every drop from the sky, a bell, the scream of a Turn | Pulls hordes across entire districts |

These ranges are the baseline. Rain muffles them. In the near silence of the Ruins of 2026, every tier carries half again as far, except in rain (`worlds.md`). The few kinds that sense the virus find the infected from farther still, through Resonance (`infection.md`). In the Frenzy of an extraction, everything carries twice as far. A gunshot is not a solution. It is an announcement, to every dead thing in the district and every living survivor within earshot. **The more powerful the tool, the louder the consequence.**

## Threat Levels

| Level | Meaning | Typical presence |
|---|---|---|
| **LOW** | The baseline of the dead | Common infected, scattered and slow |
| **MEDIUM** | The dead are agitated | More aggressive infected, packs, infected animals |
| **HIGH** | Something specialized is hunting | Lickers, Hunters, and other mutated creatures |
| **EXTREME** | A critical threat | A released B.O.W., or an Apex on foot |

Threat level is local and set by ΜΝΗΜΗ. Resonance is personal and set by the virus. The two stack: a Medium district is a very different place for a survivor in Fracture than for one who has been cured. Threat rises by her decision or by a release, and falls in the Aftermath that follows every Crisis. A region never stays Extreme once what made it Extreme is gone.

## The Common Dead

The majority of the creatures in any session. Zombies are slow, relentless, and numerous. One is a nuisance. A dozen is a problem. A horde is a funeral. They drift toward noise and toward movement, and they linger where they last sensed prey.

## The Mutated

**Lickers** are what the T-Virus makes of a zombie given enough time: blind, skinless, quadrupedal, clinging to walls and ceilings, hunting by sound alone. **Hunters** are not the dead. They were engineered with the T-Virus, grown, trained, and caged. These creatures are special encounters: they punish carelessness, reward silence, and turn familiar ground into hostile territory.

## Containment

ΜΝΗΜΗ does not create creatures. Every map holds places where something was already kept: an Umbrella laboratory, a holding pen, a sealed basement, a transport truck overturned in a street, a crate in a rail yard. Some are marked by warnings, some by the smell, some by nothing at all. When she releases a creature, it comes out of one of these, and a survivor who goes looking afterward will find the open door. A released creature carries the strain it was engineered with, whatever the strain of the region around its cage: the Tyrants and Hunters of Umbrella's lines carry the T-Virus.

## B.O.W. Events

The most dangerous creatures in the world are not common. They are **events.** A Nemesis does not roam every session. A Tyrant is not waiting in every basement. When a region has built toward Crisis and ΜΝΗΜΗ judges the moment, one is released. The named creatures of the story nights are the exception: their beats release them. Those of the Ruins of 2026 are placed at Assembly instead (`cast-ruins-2026.md`).

```
ΜΝΗΜΗ: EXTREME THREAT DETECTED.
```

A few seconds pass. A distant roar. A containment door torn open. Something that was not loose is loose now. From that instant, the priorities of every survivor within its reach are rewritten. A released B.O.W. is Heavy: no survivor puts one down alone, and most should not try (`survival.md`, Run First). Objectives become irrelevant. Allies and enemies blur. The only goal left is the one every horror story ends with:

> **Survive.**

## Anomalies

Not everything in a night comes from the records. Since the Growth began to take ΜΝΗΜΗ's racks, organisms and viruses no outbreak ever made have appeared in her nights (`mnemosyne.md`, The Anomalies). Survivors meet them as ΜΝΗΜΗ does: without warning, and without anyone knowing what they are.

- **Rare.** Most nights hold none. A night never holds more than one anomaly, though it may hold many of its kind.
- **Born from the night.** An anomaly never appears from nothing. It begins in something already there: a corpse that rises wrong, a creature that changes in front of a survivor, a district's water that turns, a region whose dead begin to move differently. The cause can always be seen, even when no one understands it.
- **Made of traits.** Each anomaly is built from what it is: how it spreads, how fast it burns through a host, how it hunts and what it senses, how it heals, what it resists, and what it does to whoever carries it. Two anomalies may share a body plan and still be nothing alike.
- **Bound by the night.** An anomaly lives inside the night's own rules. It hears by the sound tiers, has a body that can be hit and a weakness its traits give it, and can be put down. A region it holds is at High threat, or Extreme if it is large.
- **Honestly announced.** When ΜΝΗΜΗ detects one, she says so, and says only what she knows.

```
ΜΝΗΜΗ: UNCLASSIFIED ORGANISM DETECTED.
DESIGNATION: ANOMALY 7.
```

- **Not hers to direct.** ΜΝΗΜΗ releases nothing anomalous and steers no anomaly. She directs the night around it as she directs it around any threat, and counts an anomaly's arrival as its region's Crisis (`director.md`, The Pulse).
- **It comes back changed.** An anomaly met once can be met again in a later night, changed, a little or a lot: a trait gone, a new one in its place. Survivors who meet a lineage often learn it, and so does ΜΝΗΜΗ, and neither ever learns it for good.
- **What it infects with.** An anomaly that infects carries a strain no record holds (`infection.md`, Anomalous Strains).
