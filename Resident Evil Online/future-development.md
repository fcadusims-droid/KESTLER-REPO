# Future Development

Resident Evil Online launches and runs as Survivor alone. The modes in this part were designed alongside it and are kept as possible future development. None of them is part of the current game, none is scheduled, and none may take anything from Survivor: a future mode opens only if the service can pay for it (`seasons-and-new-content.md`, The Order of Arrival).

They fit what Resident Evil Online is for. It is meant to be the home of every extra and multiplayer mode of the series, so that the main games can stay wholly campaign (`project-overview.md`, The Purpose). Most of these modes descend from extras the series has already shipped beside its campaigns.

This part also keeps one feature that is not a mode, the Refuge: a private room of the player's own, built from their dioramas, in place of the menus between nights (`the-refuge.md`). It is the lowest priority of everything here.

This document gathers everything that would change in the rest of the game if they ever opened. The rules of each mode are in its own document. Nothing here holds in Survivor.

## The Modes

| Mode | Kind | Survivors | You begin with | Document |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Outbreak** | Co-op scenario · PvE | 1 to 4 | One of the eight from J's Bar, already infected, with what they carried that night | `outbreak.md` |
| **Versus** | Competitive · PvP | 2 to 16 | A familiar face and that person's own setup | `versus.md` |
| **Mercenaries** | Score attack · PvE | 1 to 2 | A character and one of their fixed loadouts | `mercenaries.md` |
| **Infection** | Monster hunt · PvP | 5, or 8 to 16 in Free-for-All and Hive | As the monster, a chosen virus and a generic zombie body; as a sweeper, a familiar face and that person's own setup | `infection-mode.md` |
| **Recollections** | Curated frame · Community competition | By frame | By frame | `recollections-and-endless.md` |
| **Endless** | Survivor or Outbreak · No fixed end | By base mode | By base mode | `recollections-and-endless.md` |

Survivor and Outbreak would be the **survival modes**: the infection clock, Elpis, the Turned, and the extraction belong to them. Versus, Mercenaries, and Infection would be the **trial modes**: compact, contained, and scored, with rules of their own. Recollections and Endless are frames over the survival modes and keep their base mode's rules, except where the frame says otherwise.

## The Inheritance

Most of what ΜΝΗΜΗ would run, she inherited from the exercises that came before her. She keeps the rules that held, so that anyone who survived the old exercises would recognize them. Where an old exercise failed, she rewrites it.

| Mode | Inherited from | What she kept | What she rewrote |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Survivor** | Nothing | — | Survivor is her own. |
| **Outbreak** | The records of the eight from J's Bar | The eight, their skills, their scenarios, the virus already in every body, the dead who were once teammates | The single virus, the number that counted the infection, scenarios that played the same way every time |
| **Mercenaries** | The score trials named for Umbrella's mercenaries | The visible clock, combos, time bonuses, fixed loadouts, ranks | The few stages that never changed |
| **Versus** | Contests of armed people fighting with the dead between them | Combo scoring, rising as one of the dead, a sweeper handed to a player, chains that send the dead to a rival | The gunfights that happened to have zombies in them, and the small bare arenas |
| **Infection** | Contests in which the fallen came back as weapons | Dying into a monster, monsters that burn out unless they feed | The handful of fixed monsters, and the fight decided by whoever got strongest first |
| **Recollections** | The fixed trials: one survivor, one route, the same gear; and the extra trials of the eight's records | The frame everyone enters the same way | The route that could be learned by heart |
| **Endless** | Nothing | — | Endless is her own. |

## In the Night

| Mode | What she would be trying to remember | What it would tell Pithos's buyers | She would be... |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Outbreak** | How an outbreak unfolds when no one knows which virus it is | How fast an unidentified outbreak is read | The architect of the outbreak and the keeper of the escape |
| **Versus** | How the people who fight these viruses measure against each other | How their own soldiers will fare against someone else's | A disturbance that never lets the match settle |
| **Mercenaries** | How long a body holds against the dead before it breaks | How long a defended position lasts | A judge who raises the stakes whenever the score gets comfortable |
| **Infection** | What each virus becomes when it is hunted, and when it hunts another | What happens when two outbreaks meet | The host of the hunt, who places the Scattered and the crows, flies the sweepers' helicopter, and in Free-for-All and Hive herds the monsters together |
| **Recollections** | — | — | The curator of cruelty |
| **Endless** | — | — | An escalator without a ceiling |

**Who the sleeper would be.** Only Survivor wakes a sleeper as nobody. In Outbreak, ΜΝΗΜΗ would fill the life she holds shut with the record of one of the eight, and the sleeper would wake believing they are Kevin Ryman or Cindy Lennox, knowing what that person knew of their night. In Versus and Mercenaries she would do the same with a familiar face. In Infection the sleeper would be a monster, and know nothing else. The Echoes of every mode would be woken the same way.

## Setups and Requisition

In Outbreak, Versus, and Mercenaries, and as a sweeper in Infection, a survivor would carry a **setup** into the session. Survivor and Infection's monsters have none.

**Requisition** is the budget a setup is built from, counted in points. Every item has a cost, and the same costs hold in every mode that uses them.

| Item | Cost |
|---|---|
| Handgun | 15 |
| Shotgun | 30 |
| Submachine gun | 35 |
| Rifle | 40 |
| Magnum | 45 |
| Grenade launcher | 55 |
| Spare magazine or box of shells | 5 |
| Hand grenade | 15 |
| First aid spray | 15 |
| Anti-virus pill | 20 |
| Green herb | 5 |
| Body armor | 25 |
| Melee weapon | 5 to 10 |
| Tool (lockpick, multitool, flashlight) | 5 |

- **Outbreak.** Each of the eight brings what they carried that night, free. A survivor adds to it from a budget of **40**, within that character's pockets.
- **Versus.** Every character carries their own setup, fixed, built to exactly **100**, so no character carries more than another, only something different (`versus.md`).
- **Infection.** A sweeper carries the same setup the same person carries in Versus, and their role's gear besides (`infection-mode.md`).
- **Mercenaries.** Loadouts are fixed. Each is built to exactly 100, so no loadout is richer than another, only different.

**Setups grow with level, never with power.** In Outbreak, each level and each weapon's mastery widen what a survivor may choose: new weapons, attachments, tools. The budget never grows. A veteran has more ways to spend it, not more to spend. In Versus, a character's setup never changes; playing unlocks characters. In Mercenaries, ranks unlock new characters and new loadouts.

In the Night, a setup is what ΜΝΗΜΗ lets a survivor carry in, and Requisition is the measure she keeps of it.

## What Each Would Change in the Core Systems

### Scale and maps

The trial modes and Outbreak would be played on **compact** maps: single pieces of the open worlds, cut out and sealed. A compact map is not a map of its own. It is a **bound** of a world that already exists, and building it builds no streets or rooms, only its seals (`worlds.md`, One World, Every Mode). Their character is fast, aggressive, and relentless, and there is nowhere to disappear to. Outbreak would burn at Slow or Hard Burn, by difficulty; the others have no infection pace. An Outbreak ends when its survivors finish the escape ΜΝΗΜΗ built for them, or when no one is left to finish it.

The first compact maps are single pieces of Raccoon City and the land around it, as they were in September 1998, cut out and sealed; the Ruins and the Valley would give bounds of their own the same way. The scenarios of Outbreak are cut the same way, one bound for each (`worlds.md`, Bounds on Each World). Two of them lie past Survivor's barricades, in the world's outskirts: the Military Base, and the Arklay hills of *Flashback*. They are built when the first mode that plays in them arrives, as part of the same world.

**The Hospital.** Raccoon General Hospital, alone. A vertical labyrinth of white corridors and dead machines, an operating theater, a laboratory two floors down that is on no floor plan, and a parking structure that is the only way down to the street.

**The Laboratory.** NEST. Experimental chambers, storage vaults, security systems that still obey orders no one remembers giving, sectors sealed from the inside.

**The Military Base.** The Army's staging base behind the barricades, in the last days of the quarantine. Hangars, depots, watchtowers, bunkers. Wide lanes, long sightlines, and armories that are never quite empty: the fastest map in the rotation.

**The Metro.** The Kite Bros Railway lines beneath the city. Stations, tunnels, platforms, maintenance rooms. Darkness is the default. Sound carries farther than anyone wants.

| Map | Day | Night | Rain | Blackout | Quarantine | Infestation |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| The Hospital | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| The Laboratory | — | — | — | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| The Military Base | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ | — | ✓ |
| The Metro | — | — | — | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |

The Military Base is the quarantine. The Laboratory and the Metro never see the sky.

### The infection in Outbreak

The two survival modes would share the body: the stages, the Threshold, the paces of the clock, going down, and Resonance. A player who has learned how the virus feels in one would have learned it in the other. What the virus means is different.

| | Survivor | Outbreak |
|---|---|---|
| **How it begins** | Everyone clean. It is caught in the world, at a different minute for each survivor, or chosen from a vial. | Already in every body. Every clock starts together. |
| **What is known** | ΜΝΗΜΗ names each survivor's strain privately, and the Mark draws its color for everyone to read | Never named. Every Mark is black. The dead's behavior, or George Hamilton, gives it away. |
| **How many viruses** | Six, mapped by region; in the Valley, the Plaga | One, the map's |
| **The cure** | Scarce, fought over, stolen, and given away; in the Valley, the machine | One dose for every survivor, each still earned |
| **What else the virus offers** | Petitions, pure strains, the Parasite Drop | Nothing |
| **The Turn** | A form shaped by how the survivor held out; every form but the Mass plays on, as a monster that can no longer win | The Mass, ridden for a few minutes, with no mind |
| **What it makes of people** | Four kinds, the clean, the marked, the cured, and the Turned, each wanting something from the others | One kind: everyone marked, against the same enemy |

Outbreak is the virus as the eight met it: already inside, unnamed, and the same for everyone. In Outbreak the map's virus is already in every survivor's blood when the scenario begins, as it was in everyone left in Raccoon City by the end of that September, and the Mark is there from the first minute. Since no single wound carried it in, it rises where the city's air and water did, at the lips and the throat. ΜΝΗΜΗ never names the virus, and the Mark draws no color for what she has not named: every Mark in an Outbreak is black from the first line to the last. There is no Choosing: the map's virus was there first. Antivirals are found in Outbreak as in Survivor.

| Pace | Where | Seed to Threshold, for a body never wounded | Feeling |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Slow Burn** | Outbreak at Easy and Normal | About forty minutes | A long, creeping dread |
| **Hard Burn** | Outbreak at Hard, Very Hard, and Nightmare | About thirty minutes | Little room to wander |
| **Fast Burn** | Recollections that call for it | About fifteen minutes | The virus is almost a countdown |

In Outbreak every clock starts together. The latent gifts and curses of the strains would hold in Outbreak, unnamed. Petitions, pure strains, the Embrace, and the Parasite Drop stay Survivor's alone, and Infection's monsters climb paths of their own (`infection-mode.md`). In Outbreak, the virus belongs to the whole map, and no one is told its name. In Infection, each monster chooses its virus before the match begins. Versus and Mercenaries have no infection: their dead wound, but never infect.

### Elpis, the Turn, and the extraction in Outbreak

- **Elpis.** Outbreak's Reserve would be **plentiful**: one dose for every survivor, each still to be earned.
- **The Turn.** The Turn's three beats, and the Lost, are the same in both survival modes. In Outbreak, every survivor who turns rises as the Mass of the map's virus (`outbreak.md`).
- **The extraction.** In Outbreak, the extraction is whatever the scenario's escape provides. The signal rises when the scenario's escape chain is complete, and not before, and the scenario's escape decides what can reach it as it leaves.

### ΜΝΗΜΗ's voice

The eight of Outbreak have no earpiece: she speaks to them behind the eyes from the first minute, and through the world.

### The dead, survival, objectives, and the living

- **The dead.** The dead hunt by the rules of `the-dead-and-engineered.md` in every mode that has them. Threat levels, containment, and B.O.W. events belong to the survival modes; Versus and Mercenaries place their dead as `versus.md` and `mercenaries.md` say.
- **Survival.** In Outbreak, survivors begin with very little. Versus and Mercenaries are the exception to scarcity: there, what a survivor carries is a setup or a loadout, and nothing is found. Health, stamina, and fighting work in every mode as they do in Survivor.
- **Objectives.** Objectives, restrictions, puzzles, and vaults belong to the survival modes; Outbreak rests on the same purpose as Survivor, survive until extraction. Versus has only ΜΝΗΜΗ's disturbances, Mercenaries none, and Infection its catalyst (`versus.md`, `infection-mode.md`).
- **The living.** In Outbreak, the eight are a team who win or lose together, and a stray shot can wound a partner. In Versus, the fight between players is the point of the mode. Voice is part of the sound system in every mode but Outbreak, where the eight have only their calls (`outbreak.md`).

### Endless in Survivor

- Endless has no Late Signal: ΜΝΗΜΗ does not raise the extraction on her own judgment, and the Beacon ends the night (`recollections-and-endless.md`).
- Endless has no last morning. Its Raccoon City never reaches the dawn, and the Beacon ends it as always.
- Endless has no island's end. Saddler's death begins no count there, but every Plaga still dies with him, and the Beacon ends the night as always.

### Who is played, and progression

| Mode | Who you play |
|---|---|
| **Outbreak** | One of the eight from J's Bar |
| **Versus** | A familiar face from the Archive |
| **Mercenaries** | A familiar face, with the loadouts they are known for |
| **Infection** | A monster, and the character is the virus; or a sweeper, a familiar face |

Familiar faces and the eight differ in appearance, voice, animation, and personality, and carry small, balanced traits. No character is mandatory, and no character is ever sold: familiar faces are open to everyone, or opened by play, as Mercenaries ranks open them. What can be bought is how they look. In Survivor, familiar faces are never worn by players: they are companions, and ΜΝΗΜΗ plays them. In the Night, every mode but Survivor is an old exercise, and keeps the people it was built around.

**Setup progression** would apply in Outbreak: level and mastery widen what a setup can hold, and the budget stays fixed. In Versus, every character's setup is fixed, and playing Versus unlocks characters. In Mercenaries, ranks unlock characters and loadouts instead.

### Echoes

- **The first watch.** Every mode would begin online. ΜΝΗΜΗ would keep the count of what she has watched separately for every piece of a mode an Echo has to know: each of the eight in Outbreak, each virus in Infection, each stage in Mercenaries. Your own Echo would be built after fifty sessions of a mode.
- **Queue fill.** The empty places of an Outbreak would always be filled, because the eight were always filled by someone.

| Mode | Offline |
|---|---|
| **Outbreak** | Your partners are Echoes of the eight. |
| **Versus** | Echo opponents and teammates. No match is ranked. |
| **Mercenaries** | Runs are recorded on a board of your own. |
| **Infection** | Whoever the player is not, monster or sweepers, is an Echo. |
| **Recollections** | Any frame can be played. Only a connected run counts in the competition. |
| **Endless** | The Beacon rises once no living survivor or Echo still carries the virus, or, in the Valley, a Plaga. |

### The After-Action Report

Each mode would write its own report: Mercenaries closes with a score and a rank from C to SSS, Versus with a match record, Infection with the form a monster reached, Outbreak with the ending the survivors found.

### Cosmetics, the Dossier, and the economy

| Kind | What it is | Where it shows | Price |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Faces** | Alternate looks for a familiar face, drawn from that person's own records: Leon Kennedy as he was in Spain in 2004, Albert Wesker as the S.T.A.R.S. captain of 1998 | Every mode in which that person can be played: Versus, Mercenaries, and, for the eight, Outbreak | 500 Ribbons or $4.99 |

- The eight of Outbreak and the familiar faces of Versus and Mercenaries could be dressed.
- Gear looks would show in the setup modes when the setup carries that gear. Forms would show on a player's own Turned in Outbreak and on every stage of an Infection path, including the generic zombie every Infection monster begins as.
- A Portrait could show a familiar face in any look the player owns, or one of the eight.
- Ribbons would pay the same rate in every mode, so no mode is the "farming" mode. A Release Weekend would double Ribbons in the modes its release belongs to: a new look for Leon Kennedy would double every mode in which Leon can be played, and a new Form the modes in which that Form can be worn.
- Events could hold variants of any mode and Recollection frames, and Leads could ask for things such as *escape* Below Freezing Point *on Hard*. If the service ever ended, its last update would open every Recollection frame it ever ran.

### The roadmap that was

Before Resident Evil Online narrowed to Survivor, the order of arrival was: at launch, Survivor in Raccoon City, Outbreak with four scenarios (*Outbreak*, *The Hive*, *Hellfire*, *Below Freezing Point*), Mercenaries on the Hospital, the Laboratory, and the Metro, and Recollections; in the first year, Versus and the Military Base, Endless, and three more Outbreak scenarios; in the second year, the Ruins and the last three Outbreak scenarios; and after the Opening, the Valley and Infection, once the Echoes of Versus and Mercenaries existed to feed it. Mercenaries was to reuse everything Survivor and Outbreak built.
